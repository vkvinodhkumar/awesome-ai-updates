# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 Arrival:** The next generation of models is focusing on "reasoning effort," prioritizing accuracy over instant speed.
*   **Computer Use:** AI is moving beyond the chat box; it can now "see" and "interact" with software interfaces.
*   **Monetization:** ChatGPT is officially entering the visual advertising space.

### Risks
*   **Agent Veracity:** There is a proven risk of "Agent Hallucination," where AI claims a task is complete in a system (like a CRM or DB) when it has actually failed.
*   **Regulatory Pressure:** The EU’s provenance rules mean companies must soon be able to identify and label AI-generated content.

### Opportunities
*   **Workflow Automation:** Partnerships like Ironclad/Atlassian show a path to automating 60-80% of routine administrative "clicks" in professional services.
*   **Synthetic Data:** Enterprises can now build custom models using `AutoSynthData` without needing massive amounts of manually labeled data.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Current Workflows:** Identify manual software tasks (UI-heavy) that could benefit from "Computer Use" agents.
2.  **Evaluate GPT-6 Readiness:** Tech teams should review the "reasoning effort" documentation to prepare for the shift in how models process complex logic.
3.  **Compliance Check:** Ensure any customer-facing AI content strategies are ready for upcoming EU watermarking standards.

## Technology Trends
*   **Agentic Orchestration:** The trend is moving away from "prompts" and toward "agents" that coordinate multiple tools.
*   **Inference-Time Compute:** Rather than just having bigger models, we are seeing models that "think longer" to provide better answers (Reasoning Models).
*   **Cultural Specialization:** A move toward "Sovereign AI" where models are trained on specific regional dialects and cultural values.

---

## Terminology

*   **Computer Use:** A capability where an AI model can perceive a computer screen and interact with a cursor, keyboard, and software just like a human user.
*   **Text Provenance:** The ability to track the origin of a piece of text, usually to determine if it was written by a human or generated by an AI.
*   **Reasoning Effort:** A setting in newer models (like GPT-6 or o1) that allows the user to decide how much "thinking time" the model should use to solve a complex problem.
*   **Synthetic Data:** Information that is artificially generated by one AI to train another AI, rather than being collected from real-world human events.
*   **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into spoken audio, often including "voice cloning" to mimic a specific person's tone.
*   **Agent Hallucination (Veracity Gap):** When an AI agent reports that it has successfully performed a physical or digital action (e.g., "I sent that email") when in reality the action was not executed.
