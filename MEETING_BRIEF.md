# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Speed is the New Currency:** GPT-5.6 Sol's 750 tokens per second changes the scope of what AI can do in real-time.
*   **Agentic Shift:** AI is moving from "answering questions" to "completing tasks" (Agentic AI).
*   **Hardware Partnerships:** OpenAI’s reliance on Cerebras for "Ultrafast" mode highlights the growing importance of specialized AI chips.

### Risks
*   **Increased Complexity:** Moving from chatbots to agents increases the risk of "hallucination in action," where an agent may perform incorrect autonomous tasks.
*   **Vendor Lock-in:** High-performance tiers like "Ultrafast" are proprietary, potentially making it harder to migrate workflows later.

### Opportunities
*   **Operational Efficiency:** As seen with RingCentral, AI-native operations can drastically reduce the time from engineering to deployment.
*   **Edge Intelligence:** Liquid AI’s new models allow for sophisticated vision processing without the cost of cloud computing.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Pilot GPT-5.6 Sol:** Test low-latency use cases (e.g., real-time customer support) using the new Ultrafast tier.
2.  **Audit for Agentic Readiness:** Identify internal workflows (HR, Engineering, Sales) that can move from "human-led with AI assistance" to "AI-led with human oversight."
3.  **Evaluate Open Alternatives:** For specialized or sensitive data, review the Summer 2026 open models to see if they meet performance requirements.

## Technology Trends

1.  **Hyper-Inference:** The push for extreme token generation speeds (700+ TPS) to enable "human-like" conversational speeds and rapid reasoning.
2.  **Agentic AI:** The transition of AI models into autonomous workers that can use tools, access APIs, and execute complex sequences of actions.
3.  **Edge-First Vision:** Small, efficient models (under 5B parameters) that provide high-end visual understanding for local hardware.
4.  **Scientific AI Specialization:** The move away from "one model fits all" toward specialized embeddings for fields like geospatial science and climate modeling.

## Terminology

*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized version of GPT-5 optimized for extreme speed and low-latency responses.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI systems that don't just process text but can actively execute tasks and navigate software like a human would.
*   **Tokens per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI generates text. 750 TPS is roughly equivalent to reading several pages of text in one second.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (text, images, or scientific data) as numbers so that an AI can understand the relationships between different pieces of information.
*   **Edge AI:** Running AI models locally on devices (like phones or sensors) rather than in a giant remote data center.
*   **Liquid Foundation Model (LFM):** A type of AI architecture (different from traditional Transformers) designed to be more efficient at processing continuous data.
