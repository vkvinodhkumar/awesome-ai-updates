# Executive Meeting Brief

- **OpenAI Silicon:** The Jalapeño chip results indicate a major shift toward proprietary hardware.
- **Model Evolution:** GPT-5.6 focuses on "price-performance," targeting the developer market.
- **Efficiency Breakthroughs:** Quantization-aware healing proves that small models are becoming increasingly competitive with large ones.

### Risks
- **Disinformation:** State-sponsored actors are actively using LLMs for covert influence; companies must monitor their API usage for reputation risk.
- **Benchmark Inflation:** Relying on standardized performance scores for ASR and LLMs may be misleading due to "benchmark optimization."

### Opportunities
- **Operational Velocity:** The Stampli case study shows a nearly 70% reduction in production time; companies should identify "bottleneck" projects for AI intervention.
- **Cost Reduction:** Newer models like GPT-5.6 and 4-bit compressed models allow for cheaper scaling of AI features.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Audit Development Tooling:** Evaluate the transition to GPT-5.6 in Kiro for software teams to capture better ROI.
2. **Explore Edge Deployment:** Investigate "Quantization-Aware" models for applications requiring low latency or local hosting.
3. **Hardware Strategy:** Monitor the availability of Jalapeño-based instances to optimize inference costs.

## Terminology

-   **Inference:** The process of an AI model running and providing an output (the "using" phase of AI, as opposed to the "training" phase).
-   **Throughput:** The amount of data or number of queries an AI system can process in a specific amount of time.
-   **Latency:** The delay or "lag" between sending a prompt and receiving a response from the AI.
-   **Quantization:** A compression technique that reduces the precision of a model's numbers (e.g., from 16-bit to 4-bit) to make it run faster and use less memory.
-   **4-bit Model:** A model that has been compressed significantly. Think of it like a low-resolution image that still manages to look as clear as a high-resolution one.
-   **Full-Precision:** A model running at its original, uncompressed mathematical detail.
-   **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** Technology that converts spoken language into text.
-   **Price-Performance:** A metric measuring how much "intelligence" or work you get for every dollar spent on AI tokens or computing.
