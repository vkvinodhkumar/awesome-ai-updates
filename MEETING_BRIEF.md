# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Vertical Specialization:** AI is solving specific, high-value industry problems (Legal/Security/Meteorology).
- **Agentic Workflow:** The industry is moving from *chatting* to *doing* (Agent-led execution).
- **Open-Weight Dominance:** High-performance models (Meta/IBM/NVIDIA) are increasingly accessible for local/on-prem deployment.

### Risks
- **Data Privacy:** Using sensitive legal and cybersecurity data with cloud-based APIs.
- **Dependency:** High reliance on specific API providers (OpenAI/NVIDIA) for core business infrastructure.

### Opportunities
- **Cost Reduction:** Dramatic reduction in operational time (e.g., SentinelOne’s weeks-to-hours shift).
- **Global Reach:** Improved multilingual capabilities (Indic project) opening new market segments.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Pilot Agentic Workflows:** Identify internal manual processes (like contract review or data analysis) to transition to autonomous agent testing.
2. **Review Open-Weight Alternatives:** Assess whether open-weight models (like Granite or Meta's Muse) can meet security requirements better than cloud-only solutions.
3. **Upskill Personnel:** Invest in AI literacy, focusing on the "Agent" paradigm rather than just "Prompting."

---

## Technology Trends

- **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE):** A design approach where a model uses only a portion of its "brain" for specific tasks, making it faster and cheaper to run.
- **Multimodality:** Systems that can process text, voice, and images simultaneously rather than needing separate tools for each.
- **Democratic Scaling:** Open-sourcing large datasets and model weights to allow smaller companies to compete with tech giants.

---

## Terminology

- **Agent:** An AI that doesn't just talk, but can perform multi-step tasks like browsing, file handling, and decision-making on its own.
- **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE):** A technique where a model is split into "expert" sub-networks, ensuring the AI is efficient by only activating the parts needed for a specific request.
- **Open-Weight:** The AI provider releases the final "brain" (weights) of the model, allowing developers to host and customize it on their own servers.
- **Tokens:** The basic units of text that an AI processes (roughly 0.75 words per token).
- **Diffusers:** A popular technical toolkit that makes it easy for developers to create images using AI models.
