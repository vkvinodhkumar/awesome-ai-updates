# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Speed is the New Frontier:** The jump to 750 tokens per second (GPT-5.6 Sol) changes the feasibility of real-time AI agents.
*   **Agentic Shift:** AI is moving from "giving advice" to "taking action" within enterprise software.
*   **Hardware Integration:** Software gains are now being driven by vertical integration with specialized hardware (e.g., OpenAI + Cerebras).

### Risks
*   **Vendor Lock-in:** As OpenAI builds more proprietary "Builder Guides" and specialized APIs, switching costs increase.
*   **Reproducibility Gap:** The Hugging Face study on ICML papers suggests many AI "breakthroughs" are difficult to replicate, which could lead to stability issues in production.

### Opportunities
*   **Edge Intelligence:** Liquid AI’s new model offers a chance to deploy sophisticated vision capabilities on local hardware without high cloud costs.
*   **Niche Data:** Using specialized embeddings (like OlmoEarth) allows for high-accuracy analysis in specific sectors like ESG or real estate.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Pilot Agentic Workflows:** Identify one "execution-based" process (e.g., automated customer resolution or dev-ops) to move from assistance to autonomous execution.
2.  **Evaluate "Ultrafast":** Assess if current user-facing AI tools would benefit from the 14x speed increase offered by the Sol model tier.
3.  **Audit for SLMs:** Determine if any cloud-based AI tasks can be moved to the "edge" using models like LFM2.5 to save on latency and cost.

## Technology Trends
*   **Inference Speed Optimization:** A shift from focusing on model size to focusing on "tokens per second" and latency.
*   **Embodied AI:** Increased toolkits for robotics (LeRobot), indicating that AI is moving into the physical world.
*   **Sovereign/Open AI:** A growing ecosystem for "Open Models" to counter the dominance of large proprietary labs.

## Terminology
*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized, high-speed version of the GPT-5.6 model optimized for rapid output.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI systems that can plan and execute multi-step tasks autonomously rather than just answering prompts.
*   **Tokens Per Second (TPS):** The speed at which an AI generates text; higher TPS means faster responses.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of converting complex data (like images or scientific readings) into a mathematical format that AI can understand.
*   **Edge AI:** Running AI models directly on local devices (like a camera or a phone) instead of on a central cloud server.
*   **Responses API:** A developer tool that allows for more precise control over how an AI model structures its answers.
*   **Liquid Foundation Models (LFM):** A type of AI architecture designed to be more efficient and adaptable than traditional models, often used for edge computing.
