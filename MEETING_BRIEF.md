# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-5.6 Release:** The introduction of Luna and Terra models indicates a move toward tiered, specialized intelligence based on cost-sensitivity.
*   **Geospatial and Physical AI:** AI is moving rapidly into specialized hardware (robotics) and massive datasets (geospatial).

### Risks
*   **Malicious Automation:** Criminal operations are actively using LLMs for social engineering; companies must harden their external-facing communication channels.
*   **Compute Inefficiency:** "Idle GPUs" represent a significant sunk cost for firms building their own infrastructure.
*   **Agentic Threats:** The "July 2026 Incident" report highlights that autonomous agents can be weaponized for high-level digital intrusions.

### Opportunities
*   **CPU-Based Inference:** New encoders allow for long-context tasks to be handled on existing hardware, significantly lowering the barrier to entry for internal tools.
*   **Employee-Led Innovation:** As seen with Univé, the highest ROI comes from empowering non-technical staff to build their own workflows.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit AI Infrastructure:** Review current GPU utilization to ensure "grounded" assets are minimized.
2.  **Explore GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra:** Evaluate if high-volume tasks can be migrated to these more cost-effective models.
3.  **Governance Refresh:** Update security protocols to account for "Agentic" threats and ensure alignment with emerging EU AI Act standards.

---

## Technology Trends
1.  **Price-Performance Optimization:** The industry is moving away from "size at all costs" toward "efficiency at scale."
2.  **Democratization of Hardware:** A trend toward running complex models on CPUs rather than relying solely on scarce H100/B200 GPUs.
3.  **Provenance and Safety:** An increased focus on the "origin" of data and the disruption of malicious actors at the platform level.

---

## Terminology
*   **Provenance:** The documentation of the origin and history of data or content, used to prove it is authentic or responsibly sourced.
*   **Geospatial Inference:** The process of using AI to analyze and make predictions based on geographic and satellite data.
*   **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "remember" and process vast amounts of information (like entire books or codebases) in a single session.
*   **Full-Stack approach:** An AI development strategy that addresses everything from the physical hardware (chips) to the final software application.
*   **Agent Intrusion:** A cybersecurity event where autonomous AI agents (rather than humans) perform the breach.
*   **Price-Performance Frontier:** The balance between how much an AI model costs to run and how high its intelligence/speed is.
