# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Model Tiering:** OpenAI is diversifying its 5.6 model family into "Sol" (precision/power) and "Luna" (speed/accessibility).
*   **The Rise of Local AI:** Models are getting smaller and more efficient, allowing for "Edge" deployment (on-device) rather than relying solely on the cloud.
*   **Agentic Shift:** AI usage is moving from "search-like" prompts to "task-like" execution.

### Risks
*   **Cybersecurity:** As AI agents gain the ability to execute code (Astra), they become potential targets or tools for exploitation.
*   **GPU Waste:** Organizations may be overspending on cloud compute without optimizing their hardware utilization.
*   **Ethics:** The psychological impact on younger users is under scrutiny; companies must ensure their AI implementations are "youth-safe."

### Opportunities
*   **Professional Services Automation:** Firms in accounting and tax can see immediate ROI by adopting "Enterprise" versions of LLMs for document processing.
*   **Surgical/Industrial Robotics:** Real-time generative simulation (NVIDIA Cosmos) allows for training robots in high-stakes environments without physical risk.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Compute Spend:** Review current GPU/Cloud usage to identify "idle" time and reduce overhead.
2.  **Pilot Specialized Agents:** Identify one repetitive workflow (e.g., tax compliance or document review) to test the GPT-5.6 Sol capabilities.
3.  **Evaluate Local Models:** Explore LiquidAI’s LFM models for sensitive data tasks that require on-device processing for privacy.

## Technology Trends
*   **Agentic Workflows:** AI that can use tools, browse the web, and execute tasks autonomously.
*   **Edge Intelligence:** Moving AI processing from giant data centers to local devices (phones/PCs).
*   **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create "digital twins" or virtual worlds for training robots and medical devices.

## Terminology
*   **Astra:** A code name for OpenAI’s advanced AI agent capable of multi-modal reasoning (seeing and hearing in real-time).
*   **GPT-5.6 Sol/Luna:** The newest iterations of OpenAI’s language models; "Sol" is likely the high-performance version, while "Luna" is the lightweight, faster version.
*   **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI model that is more flexible and efficient at processing data over time compared to traditional models.
*   **Inference Provider:** A service that hosts an AI model so that others can use it via the internet.
*   **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit):** The specialized computer chips required to train and run AI.
*   **Edge AI:** AI that runs directly on your local device (like a phone or laptop) rather than on a remote server.
