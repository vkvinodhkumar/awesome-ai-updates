# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 Astra is Live:** Early adopters are seeing 40% efficiency gains in complex document reviews.
*   **Agentic R&D:** AI developers are now using "coding agents" to automate the creation of the next generation of models.
*   **Massive Cyber Investment:** OpenAI is positioning itself as a national security partner with a $1B "Daybreak" fund.

### Risks
*   **Alignment Complexity:** Leadership at OpenAI is openly concerned about the "alien" reasoning of new models, suggesting that traditional control methods may be reaching their limit.
*   **Job Displacement in Professional Services:** The Legora case study indicates that high-level auditing and document review can now be automated with high accuracy.

### Opportunities
*   **Operational Velocity:** Leveraging coding agents for internal software development could significantly reduce time-to-market.
*   **Specialized Small Models:** The success of GRPO fine-tuning means companies can build cheap, efficient, and highly accurate internal tools without needing massive infrastructure.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Pilot GPT-6 Astra:** Evaluate current compliance, audit, and legal workflows for GPT-6 Astra integration.
2.  **Audit Cybersecurity:** Review eligibility for "Daybreak" resources if operating within essential services.
3.  **Explore Agentic Workflows:** Shift from "Chatbots" to "Agents" with persistent memory (like the Funes project) for software engineering teams.

## Technology Trends

*   **Recursive AI Improvement:** The use of AI agents to accelerate AI research indicates a transition from human-led to AI-augmented development cycles.
*   **Multimodal-Native Architectures:** Moving away from "patching" different models together toward a single model that understands text, image, and code simultaneously.
*   **Real-Time Predictive Analytics:** The shift from analyzing historical data to analyzing "streaming" data in real-time.

---

## Terminology

*   **GPT-6 Astra:** The latest high-reasoning model from OpenAI, optimized for complex document analysis and professional tasks.
*   **Coding Agents:** Autonomous AI programs designed to write, test, and debug code with minimal human intervention.
*   **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** A more efficient reinforcement learning method used to train models to follow specific structures or rules.
*   **Multimodal-native:** An AI designed from the ground up to understand different types of input (like text and video) at the same time, rather than using separate models for each.
*   **Time Series Models:** AI models specifically designed to predict future values based on past data points collected over time (e.g., stock prices or weather).
*   **Frontier AI:** The most advanced, high-scale AI models currently in existence.
