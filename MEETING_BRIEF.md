# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 & Astra:** The frontier of AI reasoning has moved toward agentic behavior and multimodal integration.
*   **Enterprise Adoption:** Major retailers (Albertsons) are now moving AI into production at scale.
*   **Open Infrastructure:** Significant tools (Olmo-core 3, Kumo Tabular) have been released to optimize specialized AI training.

### Risks
*   **Adversarial Distillation:** Competitors or bad actors may attempt to "steal" your proprietary logic by querying your AI models.
*   **Sourcing Integrity:** As agents become more autonomous, the risk of unverified actions or "hallucinated" sources increases.

### Opportunities
*   **Routine Automation:** Massive ROI is available by applying AI to the execution of routine work behind major projects.
*   **Tabular Optimization:** Leveraging new NVIDIA tools to refine financial and operational forecasting.
*   **SMB Efficiency:** Potential to scale operations without proportional increases in staffing via SBDC-supported AI frameworks.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Security Audit:** Review API usage patterns to protect against model distillation/IP extraction.
2.  **Pilot Agentic Workflows:** Begin testing "computer-use" agents for repetitive administrative tasks.
3.  **Data Strategy:** Evaluate how NVIDIA Kumo Tabular can be integrated into existing business intelligence (BI) pipelines.

## Technology Trends
1.  **Agentic AI:** Shifting from models that answer questions to agents that navigate software and complete tasks.
2.  **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE):** A trend toward model architectures that are more efficient by only activating necessary "parts" of the brain for specific tasks.
3.  **Source-Awareness:** A growing industry focus on "Verifiable AI" to combat misinformation and hallucinations.

---

## Terminology

*   **Model Distillation:** The process of training a smaller, cheaper AI model using the outputs of a larger, more expensive "teacher" model.
*   **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE):** An AI architecture where only a fraction of the model's parameters are used for any given input, making it faster and more efficient.
*   **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standard that helps AI models connect to external data sources and tools consistently.
*   **Computer-Use Agents:** AI systems capable of interacting with a computer's user interface (clicking buttons, typing, moving files) just as a human would.
*   **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into human-like spoken voice.
*   **Tabular Prediction:** AI specifically designed to analyze data organized in tables (like Excel sheets or SQL databases).
