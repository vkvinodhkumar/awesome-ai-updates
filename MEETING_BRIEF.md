# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
The arrival of **GPT-6 Astra** marks a transition to "Critical" capability levels. We are seeing the first concrete evidence of AI models performing professional-grade financial audits and complex game development with minimal human intervention.

### Risks
*   **Cybersecurity Threshold:** With Astra reaching "Critical" status, the risk of the model being used to automate cyberattacks increases, necessitating strict internal access controls.
*   **Dependency:** The 90% efficiency gains reported by users create a high risk of "capability loss" among human staff if the AI systems experience downtime.

### Opportunities
*   **Massive Efficiency Gains:** Internal departments (Marketing, Finance, Dev) should audit current "manual fix" rates to see if Astra can replicate the 50% reductions seen at Playco.
*   **Small Model Deployment:** The success of 350M parameter models suggests we can build highly specific, low-cost internal tools without expensive API overhead.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Pilot Astra:** Initiate a pilot program in the Finance or Dev Ops department to verify the 40-50% efficiency gains reported in today's news.
2.  **Infrastructure Security:** Explore OpenAI’s "Daybreak" initiative to see if our essential services/infrastructure qualify for the $1B support fund.
3.  **Data Sovereignty:** Investigate "Funes" style memory systems to ensure our proprietary code stays within our private cloud.

## Technology Trends

1.  **The Rise of the "Small Expert":** There is a move away from "one giant model for everything" toward small models (under 1B parameters) fine-tuned for specific structured tasks.
2.  **Multimodal-Native Architecture:** New models are being built from the ground up to "see" and "read" simultaneously, rather than having separate "eyes" and "brains."
3.  **Autonomous Prototyping:** AI is moving from "writing a snippet of code" to "building an entire functional prototype" with minimal human debugging.

## Terminology

*   **GPT-6 Astra:** OpenAI’s latest flagship model, optimized for high-reasoning tasks and multimodal inputs.
*   **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** A new, efficient way to train AI models by comparing a group of outputs against each other to find the best logic path.
*   **Multimodal-native:** An AI designed to process text, images, and audio all at once in its core architecture, rather than using separate plug-ins.
*   **Time Series Models:** AI specifically designed to analyze data points collected over time (like stock prices or weather) to predict future movements.
*   **TRL (Transformer Reinforcement Learning):** A method of training AI by giving it "rewards" when it completes a task correctly, similar to training a pet.
*   **Critical Level (Cybersecurity):** A designation in a safety framework indicating a model is powerful enough to significantly aid in creating or defending against high-level cyberattacks.
