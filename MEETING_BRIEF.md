# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Scientific Breakthroughs:** AI is now solving "unsolvable" math problems (Navier-Stokes) and managing quantum hardware.
*   **Architectural Efficiency:** New training methods (GRPO) allow tiny models to perform like much larger ones in structured tasks.
*   **Enhanced Creative Control:** Image generation is moving from "random prompts" to "guided reference" (Images 2.5).

### Risks
*   **Verification Latency:** While AI can propose solutions to complex math/physics, the human ability to verify these solutions may become a bottleneck.
*   **Social Impact:** The rapid adoption of AI among teens remains an under-researched area, posing potential future liability or social backlash.

### Opportunities
*   **Cost Reduction:** The ability to fine-tune 350M-parameter models provides a path to high-performance AI at a fraction of current compute costs.
*   **R&D Acceleration:** Integration of models like GPT-5.6 Sol into lab environments can significantly reduce the "trial and error" phase of physical research.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Evaluate Edge Deployment:** Assess whether small-scale models (350M) can replace larger API calls for structured data tasks to save costs.
2.  **Review AI Governance:** Update internal safety policies to reflect the "granular refusal" trend, ensuring tools remain useful without compromising safety.
3.  **Explore Autonomous R&D:** For technical departments, investigate the feasibility of integrating LLMs into automated testing or hardware calibration workflows.

---

## Technology Trends

1.  **Formal Verification:** A shift toward using "Lean" and other formal languages to ensure AI-generated math and code are 100% correct.
2.  **Small Language Models (SLMs):** Massive performance gains in models under 1 billion parameters, making "on-device" AI more viable.
3.  **Data Sovereignty:** Movement toward "User-Owned Memory" for AI agents, allowing companies to keep proprietary data on their own servers.
4.  **Native Multimodality:** Models are being built to process images and text simultaneously rather than using separate "plug-ins."

## Terminology

*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized version of the GPT-5 family optimized for high-level reasoning and scientific experimentation.
*   **Navier–Stokes Equations:** A set of complex mathematical equations that describe the motion of fluid substances (liquids and gases).
*   **Lean:** A programming language and proof assistant used to verify that mathematical proofs are logically sound.
*   **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** An efficient way to train AI by comparing a group of outputs and rewarding the best ones, used to improve model performance quickly.
*   **350M Model:** An AI model with 350 million "parameters" (variables). In the current market, this is considered a very small, lightweight model.
*   **Multimodal-native:** An AI designed from the ground up to understand text, images, and sound at the same time, rather than learning them separately.
*   **TRL (Transformer Reinforcement Learning):** A method of training AI models by giving them "rewards" for correct or desirable actions.
