# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Hardware Integration:** OpenAI is no longer just a software company; with the Jalapeño chip, they are entering the semiconductor space to control their costs and performance.
- **GPT-5.6 Launch:** The release of GPT-5.6 in the Kiro ecosystem marks a new tier of developer productivity.
- **Model Efficiency:** "Quantization-Aware Healing" suggests that smaller, cheaper models may soon rival the performance of today’s giants.

### Risks
- **Influence Operations:** State-sponsored actors are becoming more sophisticated in using LLMs to bypass traditional social media filters.
- **Benchmark "Gaming":** Relying solely on ASR benchmarks for procurement may lead to poor performance in noisy, real-world business settings.

### Opportunities
- **Cost Reduction:** Leverage the improved price-performance of GPT-5.6 for internal development tasks.
- **Edge Computing:** Utilize 4-bit "healed" models to deploy AI locally on mobile or IoT devices without losing accuracy.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Audit Security Protocols:** Review internal AI usage policies to prevent the accidental propagation of state-sponsored disinformation.
2. **Explore Custom Infrastructure:** Evaluate the potential for lower-latency applications using Jalapeño-backed inference once widely available.
3. **Upgrade Dev-Stacks:** Transition software development teams to GPT-5.6 in Kiro to capitalize on the new price-performance threshold.

## Technology Trends
- **Verticalization:** AI labs are moving toward "the full stack" (Chips -> Models -> Products) to maximize efficiency.
- **Extreme Compression:** The industry is moving toward 4-bit (and potentially lower) quantization without sacrificing intelligence.
- **Governance-First AI:** A trend toward granular admin controls for enterprise deployments.

## Terminology

- **Inference:** The process of an AI model using its training to "reason" and provide an answer to a prompt.
- **Throughput:** The amount of data/tokens an AI system can process in a given amount of time.
- **Latency:** The time delay between sending a prompt and receiving a response.
- **Quantization:** The process of reducing the precision of a model's numbers (e.g., from 16-bit to 4-bit) to make the model smaller and faster.
- **Full Stack:** In this context, controlling everything from the physical hardware (chips) to the final software product.
- **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** The technology that converts spoken language into text.
- **4-bit:** A very low level of numerical precision for AI models, typically used to run large models on small hardware.
