# Executive Meeting Brief

- **Key Developments:** Expansion of voice interaction latency reduction, enterprise ROI success stories (Circles), and the emergence of specialized local agents (LFM2.5).
- **Risks:** Increased exposure to security breaches as AI systems become more agentic (autonomous) and the ongoing legal/talent wars with legacy tech firms.
- **Opportunities:** Leveraging AI for operational efficiency in specialized sectors (telecom, surgery, geospatial) and optimizing GPU utilization to improve bottom-line performance.
- **Recommended Actions:** 
  1. Audit existing "Agent" deployments for security vulnerabilities.
  2. Implement aggressive GPU monitoring to reduce operational waste.
  3. Explore small language models (SLMs) for local, private, and cost-effective task execution.

---

## Technology Trends

1. **Edge AI/Local Models:** A shift away from "cloud-only" towards running capable, smaller models directly on local devices.
2. **Real-time Voice/Latency reduction:** Prioritizing fluid, human-like interaction speeds to move beyond current "chat-and-wait" interfaces.
3. **Agentic Workflows:** The transition from passive AI (Chatbots) to active AI (Agents that execute tasks independently).
4. **Hardware Scarcity Management:** Increased focus on "compute-efficiency" as the cost of training and inferencing models remains a major barrier.

---

## Terminology

- **Latency:** The time it takes for a system to process a request and provide a response; in AI, lower latency equals a more natural, real-time feel.
- **Agentic/Agents:** AI programs that do not just provide answers but can take actions (like browsing the web, using software, or controlling hardware) to complete a goal.
- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** A metric used to measure the average amount of revenue generated by a single user; a key indicator of business health.
- **Churn:** The rate at which customers stop using a service.
- **Edge AI:** Processing AI data locally on a device (like a phone or server) instead of sending it to a central cloud server.
- **Frontier Models:** The most powerful, state-of-the-art AI models currently available.
- **Inference:** The process of using a trained AI model to make predictions or complete tasks.
