# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 Family:** Intelligence is bifurcating into "Astra" (maximum power) and "Sol" (cost-efficient agentic power).
*   **Agentic Shift:** OpenAI’s "Dots" and H-Company’s "Holo4" show a clear industry move toward agents that use computers and manage projects autonomously.

### Risks
*   **Operational Integrity:** The incident in Australia underscores that reliance on frontier AI can create single points of failure for regional infrastructure.
*   **Verification:** As agents move faster, the risk of "black box" decision-making increases, necessitating "Source-Aware Verification."

### Opportunities
*   **80% Cost Reduction:** GPT-6.1 Sol allows for a massive scaling of automated coding and data processing tasks that were previously too expensive.
*   **Legacy Integration:** Computer-use agents (Holo4) mean businesses can automate tasks in old software that lacks modern APIs.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit API Usage:** Evaluate if current GPT-4o or GPT-5 workloads can be migrated to GPT-6.1 Sol to save costs.
2.  **Pilot "Dots":** Identify one complex, multi-week project to test OpenAI’s proactive assistants for administrative overhead reduction.
3.  **Safety Benchmarking:** Adopt the EvalEval/UK AISI standards for internal model testing to ensure performance metrics are accurate.

---

## Technology Trends

*   **Computer-Use (CU):** A move away from text-in/text-out toward models that can control a mouse and keyboard.
*   **Agentic Proactivity:** AI is moving from "waiting for a prompt" to "following a goal" (e.g., Dots).
*   **Tabular Specialization:** Specialized models for structured data are catching up to the versatility of LLMs.
*   **Benchmarking Maturity:** The industry is moving toward "Safety Cases" and reproducible science rather than just marketing claims.

---

## Terminology

*   **GPT-6 Astra:** OpenAI’s flagship, highest-performing model of the GPT-6 generation.
*   **GPT-6.1 Sol:** A high-efficiency version of GPT-6 optimized for cost and computer-interface tasks.
*   **Dots:** Proactive AI agents that can manage and execute long-term tasks without constant prompting.
*   **Computer-Use (CU) Agents:** AI models capable of looking at a computer screen and interacting with it like a human.
*   **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standard for how AI models connect to and retrieve data from various external tools and databases.
*   **Tabular Prediction:** Using AI to predict values within structured data, such as Excel sheets or SQL databases.
*   **Vision-Language Model (VLM):** An AI that can process and understand both images/video and text simultaneously.
*   **Safety Case:** A structured argument, backed by evidence, that a system is safe to operate in a specific context.
