# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **The "Agentic" Shift:** AI is moving from answering questions to executing multi-day workflows.
*   **Model Proliferation:** OpenAI has quietly expanded its GPT-6 lineup (Astra, Sol, Daybreak, Live) to target specific use cases like cybersecurity and real-time interaction.
*   **Hardware Efficiency:** Models like LFM2.5-24B prove that high-performance agents can now live on-device rather than in the cloud.

### Risks
*   **Autonomy Risks:** Long-running agents (36+ hours) require robust guardrails to prevent "hallucination loops" or unintended actions in live environments.
*   **Security:** As agents gain the ability to "Sign in" to services (e.g., Amp), they become high-value targets for credential theft.

### Opportunities
*   **Cybersecurity Efficiency:** Implementing "Daybreak" style analysis could reduce SOC (Security Operations Center) backlogs by 90%.
*   **Content Production:** Marketing and training departments can now produce 10-minute high-quality videos at a fraction of traditional costs.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Pilot Agentic Workflows:** Identify one department (e.g., IT Support or Cyber) to test OpenAI’s "Astra" or "Daybreak" models for workflow automation.
2.  **Evaluate On-Device AI:** For departments handling sensitive IP, explore LiquidAI’s LFM models to keep data off the public cloud.
3.  **Audit Long-Horizon Tasks:** Review which internal processes take "weeks" (like malware analysis or research) and assess them for AI-agent replacement.

## Technology Trends
*   **Long-Horizon Focus:** The ability for AI to stay on task for 24+ hours is the new "gold standard" for enterprise utility.
*   **Resolution Density:** In climate and image modeling, the trend is moving toward higher "granularity" (e.g., 3km weather resolution) to provide actionable local data.
*   **Modal Specialization:** Instead of one model doing everything, we are seeing a "family" approach (OpenAI’s Sol for logic, Daybreak for security, Live for voice).

## Terminology
*   **Agentic AI:** AI that doesn't just talk, but can use tools, navigate websites, and complete complex goals autonomously.
*   **GPT-6 Astra/Sol/Daybreak:** The rumored/newest iterations of OpenAI’s model family, each optimized for different tasks (Astra for general agency, Sol for logic, Daybreak for security).
*   **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** The technology that converts spoken language into text.
*   **On-Device AI:** Running AI models directly on a laptop or phone hardware rather than over the internet on a server.
*   **3 km Resolution:** In weather modeling, this refers to the size of the "grid squares" the AI calculates. Smaller squares (3km) mean much more accurate and localized forecasts.
*   **Long-Horizon Tasks:** Tasks that require an AI to remember its goal and maintain logical consistency over many hours or days.
