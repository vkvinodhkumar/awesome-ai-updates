# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **The GPT-6 Era:** The launch of Astra and Sol signifies a bifurcation of models—one for maximum power (Astra) and one for cost-effective production (Sol).
*   **Proactive Agency:** "Dots" represent a shift toward autonomous project management where the AI takes the initiative.
*   **Computer Use (CU):** Multiple articles (Sol, Holo4) highlight the move toward AI that can "use computers" rather than just "generate text."

### Risks
*   **Model Distillation:** Competitors are actively trying to "drain" intelligence from top-tier models; security protocols must be updated.
*   **Agentic Oversight:** As "dots" work autonomously, organizations must establish "human-in-the-loop" checkpoints to prevent runaway errors in complex projects.
*   **Verification:** Ensuring agents use the "right source" remains a technical hurdle for high-stakes industries.

### Opportunities
*   **SMB Efficiency:** Small businesses can now access enterprise-grade training via the SBDC partnership.
*   **Developer Margins:** GPT-6.1 Sol allows for 80% cost savings on high-intelligence API tasks, significantly improving the ROI of AI-driven products.
*   **Tabular Data ROI:** Utilizing NVIDIA Kumo can improve predictive accuracy in internal business intelligence.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit API Usage:** Transition high-volume coding and professional tasks from GPT-5/Astra to GPT-6.1 Sol to realize cost savings.
2.  **Pilot Proactive Agents:** Begin small-scale testing of "dots" for administrative and project-tracking workflows.
3.  **Evaluate Computer-Use Frameworks:** Explore Holo4 for automating internal workflows that rely on legacy software without modern APIs.

## Technology Trends
1.  **Cost-Performance Optimization:** The industry is no longer just making models *bigger*, but making them *smarter and cheaper* simultaneously.
2.  **Autonomous Operation:** Moving from "Chat" (User-led) to "Do" (AI-led).
3.  **Universal Computer Interaction:** AI is gaining the ability to use the mouse and keyboard, removing the need for custom integrations for every software.
4.  **Specialized Benchmarking:** The rise of niche leaderboards (TTS, Tabular) indicates a maturing market that demands proof of performance.

## Terminology

*   **Model Distillation:** A process where a smaller, "student" model is trained to mimic the behavior and reasoning of a larger, "teacher" model.
*   **GPT-6 Astra:** OpenAI’s most powerful flagship model, capable of high-level multimodal reasoning.
*   **GPT-6.1 Sol:** A specialized version of GPT-6 optimized for speed and cost-efficiency in professional and technical tasks.
*   **Dots:** Proactive AI agents that work autonomously across various tasks and apps without needing constant prompts.
*   **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standard that allows AI agents to interact with various data sources and tools consistently.
*   **Computer-Use Agents:** AI systems designed to interact directly with a computer's user interface (UI) to perform tasks like a human.
*   **Vision-Language Model (VLM):** An AI model that can understand and reason about both visual information (images/video) and text.
*   **Tabular Prediction:** AI-driven analysis performed on data organized in tables (like Excel or SQL databases).
