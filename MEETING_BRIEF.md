# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Agentic Shift:** AI is moving from "Chat" to "Do." Models are now executing code and generating complex financial work products (Excel/PPT).
- **Hybrid Monetization:** The introduction of ads in ChatGPT indicates a maturing market where diverse revenue streams are necessary.
- **Specialized Security:** Cybersecurity-specific models (Daybreak) are now standardized on major cloud platforms like AWS.

### Risks
- **Privacy & Ads:** Users may react negatively to data-driven ads within a conversational AI interface.
- **Infrastructure Constraints:** The push for physical infrastructure in regions like Texas highlights the massive energy and land requirements for the next generation of AI.
- **Accuracy in Execution:** As models like GPT-5.6 Sol generate financial data, the risk of "traceable" but incorrect reasoning requires strict human-in-the-loop oversight.

### Opportunities
- **Edge Deployment:** New small models (LFM2.5) allow for AI integration into hardware and IoT without relying on the cloud.
- **Cost Reduction:** Token-reduction and distillation techniques offer a path to lower the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for AI implementations.
- **Vertical Specialization:** Using Earth observation (OlmoEarth) and finance-specific (GPT-5.6 Sol) models can provide a competitive edge in niche markets.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Workflows:** Identify internal tasks that can transition from "assistance" to "agentic execution."
2.  **Evaluate Edge AI:** Explore whether Liquid AI or NVIDIA Magpie can bring AI capabilities to local devices to improve latency.
3.  **Optimize Costs:** Investigate knowledge distillation to create smaller, cheaper internal models for repetitive tasks.

## Technology Trends
1.  **Agentic AI:** The development of AI that can autonomously use tools and perform multi-step tasks.
2.  **Small Model Supremacy:** A move toward high-efficiency 3B-parameter models that rival larger counterparts in specific tasks.
3.  **Multimodal Integration:** The convergence of voice, vision, and specialized data (like geospatial) into unified workflows.
4.  **Token Efficiency:** A research focus on "thinking" more while "saying" less to save on computational costs.

---

## Terminology
- **Agentic AI:** AI that doesn't just talk but can take actions, like running code, browsing the web, or creating files to finish a job.
- **Knowledge Distillation:** A process where a large, "smart" model teaches a smaller, "faster" model how to perform a task.
- **Embeddings:** A way for computers to turn complex data (like photos of Earth or paragraphs of text) into numbers so they can be easily compared and analyzed.
- **Edge AI:** Running AI software directly on a device (like a smartphone or a camera) instead of sending the data to a far-away server.
- **Token Reduction:** Using fewer "pieces" of data to process a request, which makes the AI faster and cheaper to run.
- **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into natural-sounding human voices.
- **VLM (Vision-Language Model):** An AI model that can understand both images and text at the same time.
