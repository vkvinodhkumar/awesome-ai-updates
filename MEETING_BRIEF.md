# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 Era Begins:** The launch of Sol and Luna establishes a new performance baseline for the industry.
*   **Efficiency as a Priority:** Innovations in prompt caching and physics-based pruning show the industry is pivoting from "bigger is better" to "smarter and leaner."
*   **Localized AI:** Support for Apple MLX and llama.cpp quants suggests a massive shift toward "On-Device AI."

### Risks
*   **Geopolitical Sensitivity:** OpenAI’s direct support of Ukraine’s cyber defense may lead to retaliatory cyber-attacks or increased scrutiny of AI companies' neutrality.
*   **Benchmark Integrity:** If companies cannot reproduce "frontier" results (as addressed by UK AISI), enterprise trust in AI ROI may falter.

### Opportunities
*   **Cost Reduction:** Use the new GPT-6 caching features to reduce API spend by up to 50-80% for repetitive tasks.
*   **Market Expansion:** Partnerships like Grab's offer a blueprint for integrating AI into large-scale logistics and service workforces.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit API Usage:** Evaluate current LLM workflows to implement GPT-6's new prompt caching breakpoints to save costs.
2.  **Explore Local Deployment:** Investigate the MLX and llama.cpp support to move sensitive data processing from the cloud to local, secure hardware.
3.  **Tiered Model Strategy:** Adopt a "Sol/Luna" approach—use high-reasoning models for strategy and lightweight models for routine automation.

## Technology Trends
*   **Model Tiering:** Separating "Reasoning" models from "Utility" models.
*   **Physics-Informed ML:** Using concepts from thermodynamics and physics (like the Ising model) to optimize neural networks.
*   **Democratized Access:** A heavy focus on Southeast Asia and developer academies to diversify the AI workforce.

## Terminology

*   **Prompt Caching:** Storing previously processed text so the AI doesn't have to "re-read" it, saving time and money.
*   **Speaker Diarization:** The process of partitioning an audio stream into segments according to who is speaking ("Who spoke when").
*   **Quantization (Quants):** A technique to shrink AI models by reducing the precision of their internal numbers, allowing them to run on weaker hardware.
*   **Model Pruning:** Removing unnecessary parts of a neural network to make it smaller and faster without losing accuracy.
*   **Ising Optimization:** A mathematical method borrowed from physics used to find the best configuration of a system (in this case, which blocks of an AI to remove).
*   **MLX:** A specialized machine learning framework designed specifically for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3 chips).
