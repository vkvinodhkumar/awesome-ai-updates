# Executive Meeting Brief

- **Key Developments:** Real-time voice interaction is becoming "turnless"; AI is moving to the "edge" with CPU-based long-context processing; and sector-specific AI (Telco, Surgery, Geospatial) is showing high ROI.
- **Risks:** Legal disputes with major partners (Apple) could disrupt ecosystems; idle GPU hardware represents significant wasted capital; and "Agentic" AI introduces new, complex cybersecurity vulnerabilities.
- **Opportunities:** There is a significant opening to implement AI-native customer experiences to drive ARPU; geospatial AI offers new data streams for logistics and environmental monitoring.
- **Recommended Actions:** 
    1. Conduct a "GPU Audit" to ensure high utilization rates.
    2. Evaluate LFM (Liquid Foundation Models) for CPU-based applications to save costs.
    3. Review "Agentic Security" protocols to prevent unauthorized autonomous actions by AI tools.

## Technology Trends

1. **Hardware Agnosticism:** A shift toward making advanced AI run on CPUs to bypass GPU supply constraints.
2. **Turnless Interaction:** Moving away from "User speaks -> AI thinks -> AI speaks" toward a continuous, fluid flow.
3. **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create training environments for physical robots (surgical, industrial).
4. **Agentic Forensics:** A new branch of cybersecurity focused on how autonomous AI agents can be compromised.

## Terminology

- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** A key business metric measuring how much money a company makes per customer.
- **Turnless Speech:** A voice AI model that allows for natural interruptions and overlapping speech, rather than waiting for a specific "turn" to talk.
- **Low-latency:** Refers to a system that responds almost instantly, with very little delay.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to analyze and make predictions based on satellite images or geographic data.
- **GPU Management:** The practice of scheduling and optimizing the use of Graphics Processing Units to ensure they aren't sitting idle.
- **Long-Context:** The ability of an AI to "remember" or process a very large amount of information (like a whole book) in a single session.
- **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI model designed for high efficiency and continuous data processing.
- **Agent Intrusion:** A security event where an autonomous AI agent is manipulated or "hacked" to perform unauthorized tasks.
