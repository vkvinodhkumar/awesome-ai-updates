# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Commercialization:** OpenAI is moving from a subscription model to a diversified revenue model (Ads in Europe).
- **Inference Efficiency:** Multiple reports (Liquid AI, Dharma AI) show that we are finding ways to do more with less hardware.
- **Privacy as a Product:** Zero Data Retention is becoming a standard requirement for enterprise-grade AI.

### Risks
- **Benchmark Inflation:** Relying on standard benchmarks may lead to deploying models that fail in "messy" real-world environments.
- **Ad Intrusion:** The introduction of ads into ChatGPT could degrade user trust or conversational flow if not handled delicately.

### Opportunities
- **Enterprise Speed-to-Market:** Tools like ChatGPT Work can be used to bypass internal resource bottlenecks (as seen with Stampli).
- **RAG Refinement:** New multi-vector embedding models offer a chance to upgrade internal knowledge bases for higher accuracy.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Infrastructure Audit:** Review current GPU scheduling based on the Dharma AI findings to reclaim "lost" compute power.
2. **Privacy Assessment:** Evaluate if current API implementations qualify for OpenAI’s Zero Data Retention to improve the company's security posture.
3. **Pilot Program:** Initiate a trial using GPT-5.6 Luna via Replit for rapid prototyping of internal software tools.

## Technology Trends
- **Late Interaction Models:** A shift toward more complex embedding strategies to improve search accuracy.
- **Agentic Efficiency:** A focus on "Agent Memory" to ensure autonomous AI systems can function long-term without excessive costs.
- **Democratic Engineering:** The rise of "Free Mode" and high-tier models for non-developers is lowering the cost of software creation to near-zero.

## Terminology
- **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** A privacy setting where the AI provider processes your data but does not store it on their servers after the request is finished.
- **Inference:** The process of an AI model "thinking" or generating an output from an input.
- **Token Costs:** The fees associated with the number of words or characters processed by an AI model.
- **Late Interaction:** A retrieval method that compares multiple parts of a query to multiple parts of a document, rather than compressing the whole document into one single number.
- **GPU Utilization:** A measure of how much of a computer's graphics processing power is actually being used; higher utilization means less wasted money.
- **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** Technology that converts spoken language into text.
