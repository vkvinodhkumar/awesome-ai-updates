# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **The "GPT-6" Era:** We are entering a phase where AI models allow for "tunable reasoning," meaning we can adjust how hard the AI "thinks" about a problem.
- **Workflow Compression:** Enterprise case studies show a 7x to 10x reduction in time-to-completion for complex administrative and financial tasks.
- **Open-Source Specialization:** Tools are becoming more specialized (e.g., AstaBrief for reports, Kumo for spreadsheets) rather than just general-purpose.

### Risks
- **Verification Gap:** As models generate more content, the risk of "well-phrased hallucinations" remains. Source-aware verification (as seen in the MCP research) is becoming a mandatory requirement.
- **Dependency on Closed Ecosystems:** While open-source is growing, many efficiency gains are currently tied to proprietary models (OpenAI GPT series).

### Opportunities
- **Synthetic Data Training:** Using tools like AutoSynthData, we can now train internal AI agents without the liability of using sensitive raw data.
- **Small Business Scalability:** AI allows smaller teams to handle "big company" administrative loads, providing an opportunity for rapid expansion with low headcount growth.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Workflows:** Identify "high-friction" administrative tasks (like trade validation or licensing) for automation using reasoning-focused models.
2.  **Evaluate Tabular Tools:** Review NVIDIA’s Kumo Tabular for internal data science teams to improve forecasting accuracy.
3.  **Implement Source Verification:** Ensure any AI agents deployed for internal use utilize "source-aware" protocols to maintain data integrity.

---

## Technology Trends

1.  **Reasoning Effort Tuning:** Moving away from "one-size-fits-all" responses toward "thinking" models where latency/cost can be traded for better logic.
2.  **Synthetic Data Generation:** A shift from collecting data to *generating* high-quality, synthetic data to train enterprise-specific models.
3.  **Hyper-Specialization:** The emergence of models dedicated specifically to tabular data, report generation, or voice cloning rather than general LLMs.
4.  **Operational AI:** AI is transitioning from a "chatbot" to a "workflow engine" integrated into the core architecture of enterprise software.

## Terminology

- **GPT-6 / GPT-5.6:** Next-generation Large Language Models (LLMs) from OpenAI featuring enhanced reasoning and logic capabilities.
- **Reasoning Effort:** A setting that allows a user to control how much computational time/power a model spends "thinking" before providing an answer.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standardized way for AI models to connect to external data sources and tools securely.
- **Synthetic Training Data:** Artificial data created by AI that mimics the characteristics of real-world data, used to train other models.
- **Tabular Prediction:** The use of AI to analyze and predict outcomes based on data organized in rows and columns (spreadsheets).
- **Voice Cloning:** The process of using AI to create a synthetic version of a specific human's voice.
- **Codex:** A specialized AI model designed to understand and generate computer code.
