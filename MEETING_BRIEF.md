# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Privacy-First AI:** Zero Data Retention is becoming the standard for enterprise-grade API usage.
*   **Monetization Shift:** ChatGPT is officially an advertising platform, expanding rapidly across Europe.
*   **Inference Speed:** New architectures (Liquid Models) are significantly outperforming standard LLMs in speed.

### Risks
*   **Ad Bias:** The introduction of ads into ChatGPT may influence the neutrality of information provided to users.
*   **Benchmark Gaming:** Reliability of AI models may be lower than reported due to "over-optimization" for tests.
*   **Data Silos:** As companies move to "Private Safety Processing," auditing for AI bias may become more difficult.

### Opportunities
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Following Stampli’s lead, departments can reduce production timelines by over 60% using tailored AI workflows.
*   **RAG Improvements:** Implementing "Late Interaction" embedding models can drastically improve the performance of internal knowledge bases.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Review API Architecture:** Ensure that all sensitive workflows are utilizing Zero Data Retention (ZDR) endpoints.
2.  **Evaluate Advertising Presence:** Marketing teams should investigate the "ChatGPT Ads" ecosystem to ensure brand visibility in AI-driven discovery.
3.  **Compute Audit:** Explore GPU scheduling optimizations to reduce infrastructure overhead by up to 30%.

## Technology Trends
*   **Shift from Transformers:** The rise of Liquid Foundation Models suggests we are moving toward more efficient, non-transformer architectures.
*   **Agentic Memory:** A focus on how AI "remembers" tasks is moving agents from "chatbots" to "autonomous employees."
*   **Efficiency over Scale:** The focus is shifting from "bigger models" to "faster, smarter, and cheaper models" (e.g., GPT-5.6 Luna, LFM2.5).

## Terminology

*   **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** A security setting where the AI provider does not save or store any of the data sent to the model after the request is processed.
*   **Liquid Foundation Models (LFM):** A type of AI architecture that is more fluid and efficient than standard models, allowing for faster processing and lower power consumption.
*   **Late Interaction (Multi-Vector):** A sophisticated search method that looks at the relationship between multiple words in a search query and a document, leading to much more accurate results than simple keyword matching.
*   **Inference:** The process of an AI model actually "thinking" and generating an answer after it has been trained.
*   **GPU Utilization:** A measure of how much of a computer’s "brain power" (Graphics Processing Unit) is actually being used; higher utilization means less wasted money.
*   **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** The technology that converts spoken language into text.
