# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Agentic Shift:** OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra moves beyond text generation into "computer use," signifying a new era of software automation.
- **Quantum Acceleration:** AI is now successfully managing quantum hardware, moving from theoretical help to practical lab operation.
- **Efficient Specialization:** New methods (GRPO) and models (Granite) allow for high performance at lower costs and smaller scales.

### Risks
- **Regulatory Uncertainty:** The "policy window" mentioned by Lehane suggests that current AI implementations may soon face new compliance requirements.
- **Data Sovereignty:** As AI agents gain "memory," the risk of sensitive corporate data being stored in external clouds increases.
- **Model Refusal:** Standard safety protocols may inadvertently limit the utility of models for legitimate business research.

### Opportunities
- **Operational Cost Reduction:** Using GPT-6 Astra for "computer use" can automate repetitive back-office tasks.
- **Predictive Analytics:** IBM’s new Granite model offers a "commercial-friendly" way to improve supply chain and financial forecasting.
- **Small Model Deployment:** Leveraging 350M parameter models for structured data tasks can drastically reduce API costs.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Pilot GPT-6 Astra:** Identify one high-volume manual software process to test Astra’s "computer use" capabilities.
2. **Review Data Privacy for Agents:** If deploying coding agents, explore self-hosted memory solutions like Funes to protect IP.
3. **Explore Time-Series Models:** Technical teams should evaluate IBM’s Granite model for internal forecasting needs.
4. **Monitor AI Policy:** Task the legal team with tracking the "safety standards" OpenAI is advocating for to ensure future compliance.

## Technology Trends
1. **Agentic Computer Use:** Moving from "chatting" to "doing" by interacting with software GUIs.
2. **Scientific Autonomy:** AI models acting as autonomous lab assistants in high-complexity fields like quantum physics.
3. **Structured Small Models:** A move toward training very small models to perform specific, high-accuracy tasks (like code or JSON generation) to save on compute.
4. **Granular Alignment:** A shift in safety research from "blocking topics" to "filtering specific harms."

## Terminology
- **Computer Use:** The ability of an AI model to "see" a screen, move a cursor, and type into applications like a human.
- **AI Alignment:** The process of ensuring an AI's goals and behaviors match human values and intentions.
- **Time Series:** A sequence of data points indexed in time order (e.g., daily sales, hourly temperature).
- **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** A newer, more efficient way to train AI models to follow specific instructions or formats without needing as much computing power as previous methods.
- **Multimodal-native:** An AI built from the ground up to understand multiple types of data (text, images, audio) at once, rather than bolting them together later.
- **Qubits:** The basic unit of information in quantum computing (the quantum version of a traditional "bit").
