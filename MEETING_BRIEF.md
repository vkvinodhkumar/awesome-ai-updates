# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Speed as a Feature:** The jump to 750 tokens/second (GPT-5.6 Sol) changes the UX paradigm from "typing" to "appearing."
- **Agentic Shift:** Enterprises are no longer just "using" AI; they are "employing" it to perform autonomous tasks.
- **Hardware Integration:** The OpenAI/Cerebras partnership highlights that software gains are now increasingly tied to specialized AI hardware.

### Risks
- **Model Reproducibility:** The ICML study suggests that a significant portion of AI research may be difficult to replicate, posing a risk to firms relying on unverified "frontier" techniques.
- **Policy Lag:** As AI moves to "execution mode," current legal frameworks may not be equipped to handle autonomous AI errors.

### Opportunities
- **Edge Deployment:** LiquidAI’s LFM2.5 allows for sophisticated vision capabilities in low-bandwidth environments.
- **Niche Specialization:** Tools like OlmoEarth allow firms to build highly specialized geospatial AI applications.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Infrastructure Audit:** Evaluate if current workflows would benefit from "Ultrafast" inference or if "Edge" deployment is more cost-effective.
2. **Transition to Agents:** Pilot programs should shift from "Chatbot" interfaces to "Agentic" workflows that can execute API calls and complete tasks autonomously.
3. **Open vs. Closed Strategy:** Review the 2026 State of Open Models to determine if fine-tuning an open-source model is now more viable than a proprietary subscription.

## Technology Trends
1. **Agentic Workflows:** Moving from LLMs as responders to LLMs as actors.
2. **Hardware-Software Co-design:** Using specialized chips (Cerebras) to achieve "Ultrafast" speeds.
3. **Physical AI:** The convergence of LLMs and robotics (LeRobot) to bring intelligence to the physical world.
4. **Spatial Intelligence:** AI trained specifically on geospatial and Earth-observation data.

## Terminology
- **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specific variant of the GPT-5.6 model optimized for extreme inference speed.
- **Tokens Per Second (TPS):** The speed at which an AI generates text; higher TPS allows for smoother, real-time interactions.
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that can independently plan, use tools, and execute multi-step tasks to reach a goal.
- **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like text or images) as numbers so that an AI can understand the relationships between different pieces of information.
- **Edge AI:** Running AI models directly on local devices (like a phone or robot) rather than on a central cloud server.
- **Open Models:** AI models whose "weights" and architecture are shared publicly for anyone to use or modify.
