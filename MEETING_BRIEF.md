# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Shift to Agents:** AI is moving from "answering" to "doing" (Agentic AI).
*   **Model Specialization:** Specialized models for cybersecurity (Daybreak) and Earth science (OlmoEarth) are becoming the norm.
*   **Monetization Shift:** OpenAI is moving toward ad-supported tiers, potentially changing the user experience for non-paying customers.

### Risks
*   **Infrastructure Reliability:** The push for massive data centers (e.g., in Texas) places a strain on energy grids.
*   **Security Latency:** While AI security models are improving, the speed of offensive AI may still outpace defensive deployment.

### Opportunities
*   **Edge Computing:** New small models (Liquid AI, NVIDIA Magpie) allow for local AI deployment, reducing data privacy risks and cloud costs.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** Advances in knowledge distillation and token reduction (IBM) mean organizations can do more with less compute.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Workflows:** Identify manual processes that can transition from "human-assisted" to "agent-executed."
2.  **Evaluate Amazon Bedrock:** If cybersecurity is a priority, test the Daybreak models for automated threat hunting.
3.  **Explore Small Models:** Investigate LFM or Magpie for internal tools that require low latency or high privacy.

## Technology Trends
*   **The Rise of Agentic AI:** Moving beyond chatbots to systems that interact with APIs and software to complete tasks autonomously.
*   **Edge AI (On-Device):** A growing trend toward smaller, "efficient" models (under 3B parameters) that run locally on hardware.
*   **Sustainable Distillation:** Focusing on shrinking large models into smaller, cheaper versions (Knowledge Distillation) to save on operational costs.

## Terminology
*   **Agentic AI:** AI that can take actions and complete multi-step tasks independently, rather than just generating text.
*   **Codex:** An OpenAI model specialized in translating natural language into computer code.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like words or images) as numbers so an AI can understand the relationships between them.
*   **Knowledge Distillation:** A technique where a small "student" model is trained to mimic a large "teacher" model to save costs.
*   **Tokens:** The basic units (chunks of characters) that AI models process; reducing tokens makes AI faster and cheaper.
*   **Vision-Language Model (VLM):** An AI that can understand and describe both images and text simultaneously.
*   **Edge AI:** Running AI algorithms locally on a device (like a smartphone) rather than on a distant cloud server.
*   **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into natural-sounding human speech.
