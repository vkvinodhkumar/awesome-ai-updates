# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Model Evolution:** GPT-5.6 is now targeting the developer market with a focus on "price-performance," indicating a shift from "raw power" to "economic utility."
- **Privacy Standards:** Zero Data Retention (ZDR) is becoming a standard requirement for enterprise-grade AI.

### Risks
- **Geopolitical Interference:** State actors are actively using AI for covert influence, which could lead to platform instability or unintended brand associations.
- **Benchmark Inflation:** Technical "benchmarks" may not always reflect real-world AI performance, potentially leading to poor procurement decisions.

### Opportunities
- **Operational Speed:** As demonstrated by Stampli, AI can reduce production timelines by over 60% in specific workflows.
- **Cost Reduction:** New inference techniques (LFM2.5) and memory optimization (IBM Research) are making it cheaper to run complex AI at scale.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Audit Developer Tools:** Evaluate if switching to GPT-5.6 via Kiro can reduce software development costs.
2. **Review Data Privacy:** Ensure all API-based AI projects utilize Zero Data Retention (ZDR) where sensitive customer data is involved.
3. **Explore Workflow Automation:** Review the Gradio workflow guides to see if internal prototypes can be more effectively deployed to staff.

---

## Technology Trends
1. **Efficiency Engineering:** The industry is moving away from "bigger is better" toward "faster and cheaper" (Inference speedups and price-performance models).
2. **AI Transparency & Safety:** There is an increased focus on the long-term impact of AI (AI Futures) and the active policing of malicious use (OpenAI’s disruption of Russian campaigns).
3. **Agentic Optimization:** Research is shifting toward how "agents" (AI that acts on your behalf) manage memory and resources.

---

## Terminology

- **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** A privacy setting where the AI provider does not store any of the data sent to the model after the request is processed.
- **Inference:** The process of an AI model "thinking" or generating an output based on an input. Faster inference means faster responses.
- **Frontier Models:** The most advanced, highest-capacity AI models currently available (e.g., GPT-4, GPT-5.6).
- **Multi-Vector / Late Interaction:** A sophisticated way for AI to search through data that looks at multiple points of context rather than just one, leading to more accurate search results.
- **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** Technology that converts spoken language into text.
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that are designed to perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously, rather than just answering questions.
