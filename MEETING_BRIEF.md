# Executive Meeting Brief

- **Key Developments:** OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 release marks a new baseline for developer tools; Zero Data Retention (ZDR) is now a standard for frontier models.
- **Risks:** Sophisticated state actors (Russia) are actively using AI for influence operations; companies must be vigilant about the provenance of information.
- **Opportunities:** Massive efficiency gains in software development (via GPT-5.6) and product launches (68% reduction in hours) are now achievable.
- **Recommended Actions:** 
    1. Review API data retention policies to enable ZDR for sensitive projects.
    2. Audit internal developer workflows to integrate GPT-5.6 for cost-saving code reviews.
    3. Evaluate "Late Interaction" embedding models for existing RAG systems to improve search accuracy.

## Technology Trends

1. **Inference Efficiency:** The focus is shifting from making models "bigger" to making them "faster" (e.g., Liquid AI’s 3.2x boost).
2. **Privacy-First Enterprise AI:** ZDR and Private Safety Processing are becoming the "gold standard" for B2B AI offerings.
3. **Agentic Workflows:** Moving away from single chatbots toward multi-step, "wired" workflows (Gradio) and memory-optimized agents (IBM).
4. **Information Integrity:** AI providers are taking on the role of digital security firms to combat AI-generated misinformation.

## Terminology

- **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** A privacy setting where the AI provider does not store or save any of the data sent to their models.
- **Inference:** The process of a trained AI model actually generating a response or prediction from new data.
- **Multi-Vector (Late Interaction):** A method of search that looks at many different "points of meaning" in a sentence at once, leading to more accurate search results than traditional methods.
- **Hidden Markov Model (HMM):** A mathematical way to model processes that change over time, used here to help AI agents manage their memory more efficiently.
- **Influence Campaign:** A coordinated effort (often by a government) to spread specific information—often misleading—to change public opinion.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** The process of giving an AI model access to a specific set of documents to ensure its answers are based on factual, up-to-date information.
