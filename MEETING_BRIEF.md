# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Speed is the New Frontier:** With the 14x speed increase in GPT-5.6 Sol, latency is no longer a primary bottleneck for complex workflows.
*   **The Rise of Agents:** The focus has shifted from "Chat" to "Agents"—systems that can take actions, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks autonomously.
*   **Commercial Maturation:** Major AI players are professionalizing their sales and revenue leadership to better serve the Fortune 500.

### Risks
*   **Infrastructure Lock-in:** Rapidly adopting proprietary modes like "Ultrafast" may lead to dependency on specific hardware/software ecosystems (e.g., Cerebras/OpenAI).
*   **Operational Complexity:** Moving to agentic AI requires higher levels of oversight and "guardrailing" to prevent autonomous errors.

### Opportunities
*   **Hyper-Automation:** The ability to execute 750 tokens per second allows for real-time customer service and automated software engineering at scale.
*   **Edge Intelligence:** New 3B-parameter models allow for high-tier vision capabilities directly on hardware, reducing data transit costs.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Pilot Agentic Workflows:** Transition internal pilot programs from simple "help bots" to "action agents" that can interface with internal APIs.
2.  **Evaluate Edge Use-Cases:** Assess if LFM2.5-VL-3B can replace cloud-based vision APIs to save costs and improve privacy.
3.  **Review AI ROI:** With the new CRO at OpenAI, expect changes in enterprise pricing; now is the time to lock in favorable enterprise agreements.

## Technology Trends
1.  **Token Velocity:** We are moving away from "reading speed" AI to "machine-processing speed" AI (750+ tokens/sec).
2.  **Embodied AI:** The convergence of LLMs and robotics (LeRobot) is making physical automation more accessible.
3.  **Scientific Verification:** The industry is self-correcting with massive reproduction studies to ensure research reliability.
4.  **Domain-Specific Embeddings:** A shift toward specialized data (like OlmoEarth) rather than one-size-fits-all models.

---

## Terminology
*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** The latest iteration of the GPT-5 series, optimized for high-speed "Ultrafast" inference.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI that does not just talk, but takes actions and executes tasks using external tools and software.
*   **Tokens per second (tps):** A measure of how fast an AI generates text. 750 tps is roughly equivalent to 500-600 words every second.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like words or images) as numbers so that computers can understand the relationship between them.
*   **Edge AI:** Running AI models locally on a device (like a phone or a camera) rather than on a massive server in the cloud.
*   **Reproducibility:** The ability for an independent team to get the same results as the original researchers, proving the science is sound.
