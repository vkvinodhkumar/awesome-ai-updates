# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 Preparation:** OpenAI is officially preparing the market for the GPT-6 family, focusing on "tunable reasoning."
*   **Ad Monetization:** ChatGPT is moving into the visual advertising space, creating a new competitive front against Google Search.
*   **Efficiency Benchmarks:** Financial sector case studies (Chatham) are hitting "holy grail" metrics (85%+ time reduction).

### Risks
*   **Compliance Deadlines:** The EU's strict text provenance rules require immediate technical implementation to avoid heavy fines.
*   **Agent Reliability:** The "ThinkingBox" problem highlights that agents may report success while failing the underlying task, necessitating better verification layers.

### Opportunities
*   **Synthetic Data Training:** Using tools like AutoSynthData to train proprietary internal models without compromising customer privacy.
*   **Tabular Forecasting:** Leveraging NVIDIA Kumo to gain an edge in financial forecasting and supply chain management using existing spreadsheet data.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Workflows:** Identify routine validation tasks (similar to Chatham’s trade checks) for immediate AI integration.
2.  **Evaluate Reasoning Needs:** Determine which internal projects require "GPT-6 level" reasoning vs. cheaper, faster "AstaBrief style" summarization.
3.  **Review Provenance Strategy:** If operating in the EU, begin testing OpenAI’s watermarking tools for any public-facing AI content.

## Technology Trends
*   **Tunable Reasoning:** The ability to tell a model how much "effort" to put into a specific prompt, balancing cost vs. intelligence.
*   **Agentic Verification:** Moving from models that just "chat" to agents that "do," with external systems verifying their work.
*   **Synthetic-First Training:** A shift toward training enterprise AI on high-quality, artificially generated data rather than messy, private real-world data.
*   **Multimodal Ad Tech:** The integration of visual ads directly into AI conversational flows.

---

## Terminology
*   **Text Provenance:** The ability to track the origin of a piece of text to prove whether it was written by a human or an AI.
*   **Reasoning Effort:** A setting in next-gen models (like GPT-6) that dictates how many "thinking steps" the AI takes before answering.
*   **Synthetic Data:** Data that is generated by an AI model rather than collected from real-world events, used to train other AI models.
*   **Tabular Data:** Structured data organized in rows and columns, like a spreadsheet or a database table.
*   **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Technology that converts written text into spoken audio, often involving "voice cloning" to mimic a specific person.
*   **Agentic Workflow:** A process where an AI "agent" is given a goal and independently chooses which tools and steps to take to achieve it.
