# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **The GPT-6 Era:** The arrival of Astra and Sol models sets a new baseline for reasoning and coding.
- **Agentic Shift:** OpenAI’s "Dots" and Holo4 represent a move toward autonomous agents that "do" rather than just "talk."
- **Cost Reduction:** A 80% price reduction for high-tier intelligence (via GPT-6.1 Sol) changes the ROI calculation for AI integration.

### Risks
- **Autonomous Misalignment:** Proactive agents (Dots) could make errors if not monitored, necessitating the "safety cases" OpenAI is developing.
- **Source Integrity:** As agents act on web data, the risk of acting on misinformation increases (addressed by Source-Aware Verification).
- **Security:** "Computer-use" agents require strict permissioning to prevent unauthorized system access.

### Opportunities
- **Legacy Automation:** Using computer-use agents (Holo4) to automate tasks in software that lacks modern APIs.
- **Small Business Scaling:** Leveraging OpenAI’s training partnerships to upskill staff quickly.
- **Financial Optimization:** Migrating high-volume coding or data tasks to the GPT-6.1 Sol model to slash operational costs.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit API Usage:** Identify high-cost workflows that can be transitioned to GPT-6.1 Sol to save costs.
2.  **Pilot Proactive Agents:** Begin a small-scale trial of "Dots" for project management or internal coordination.
3.  **Review Safety Protocols:** Align internal AI deployment with the new "safety case" guidelines to ensure ethical and secure usage.

## Technology Trends

1.  **Agentic Workflow:** Moving away from single-prompt interactions toward long-running, proactive AI "teammates."
2.  **Computer-Use Models:** Models are being trained specifically to interact with Graphical User Interfaces (GUIs).
3.  **Intelligence Democratization:** High-tier AI is becoming a commodity through aggressive price cuts and localized training for small businesses.
4.  **Verification-Centric AI:** A move toward "Source-Aware" systems to combat the trust issues associated with LLM hallucinations.

## Terminology

- **GPT-6 Astra:** OpenAI's latest flagship model, representing the peak of current reasoning and multimodal capabilities.
- **GPT-6.1 Sol:** A specialized version of GPT-6 optimized for coding and computer use at a significantly lower cost.
- **Dots:** Proactive AI assistants capable of managing tasks and projects autonomously over time.
- **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into audible, human-like speech.
- **Computer-Use Agents:** AI models capable of "seeing" a computer screen and "using" the mouse and keyboard to perform tasks.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standard for how AI agents interact with and retrieve information from various data sources.
- **Tabular Prediction:** Using AI to analyze and predict trends within structured data (like Excel sheets or SQL databases).
- **Vision-Language Model (VLM):** An AI model that can understand and process both images and text simultaneously.
