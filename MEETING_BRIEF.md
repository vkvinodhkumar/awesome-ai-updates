# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
The arrival of **GPT-5.6 Sol** and **Ultrafast mode** is the headline. We are seeing inference speeds (750 tokens/sec) that make AI-driven software feel as fast as local code. Additionally, the move toward **Agentic AI** means models are no longer just "talking"; they are "doing."

### Risks
*   **Execution Risk:** As models move from "assistance" to "execution," the potential for autonomous errors in enterprise workflows increases.
*   **Infrastructure Lock-in:** The 14x speed increase is hardware-dependent (Cerebras), which may lead to specialized pricing tiers and vendor dependency.

### Opportunities
*   **Edge Intelligence:** With LFM2.5-VL, we can now deploy vision-capable AI on local hardware, reducing data privacy concerns and latency.
*   **Cost Optimization:** IBM’s work on token reduction and OpenAI’s smarter model selection via the Responses API allow for high-performance AI at a lower price point.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Pilot GPT-5.6 Sol:** Test Ultrafast mode for customer-facing support bots to achieve near-zero latency.
2.  **Audit Workflows for "Agentic" Potential:** Identify manual engineering or ops tasks that can be automated using the new builder's guide for agents.
3.  **Evaluate Edge Vision:** For physical security or field operations, explore LFM2.5-VL for on-device processing.

---

## Technology Trends
1.  **Hyper-Inference:** The race is no longer just about model size, but about "tokens per second."
2.  **Agentic Shift:** Transitioning from Large Language Models (LLMs) to Large Action Models (LAMs) that can interact with APIs and execute code.
3.  **Scientific Transparency:** A growing industry-wide movement to verify and reproduce AI research results to build trust.
4.  **Specialized Embeddings:** Moving away from general-purpose models toward domain-specific embeddings (e.g., Geospatial/Earth data).

---

## Terminology

*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized, high-speed variant of the GPT-5.6 model optimized for rapid response.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI systems that don't just answer questions but can perform tasks, use tools, and make decisions to reach a goal.
*   **Tokens per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI generates text. Higher TPS results in a smoother, more human-like speed.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like words or images) as numbers so a computer can understand the relationship between different concepts.
*   **Edge AI:** Running AI models directly on a local device (like a camera or phone) rather than in a giant data center.
*   **Responses API:** A tool for developers that allows more granular control over how the AI generates and delivers its answers.
*   **Vision-Language Model (VLM):** An AI that can "see" (process images) and "talk" (process text) at the same time.
