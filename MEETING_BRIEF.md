# Executive Meeting Brief

- **Key Developments:** Proliferation of specialized AI models (Law/Sales) and the maturing of local, consumer-hardware-friendly infrastructure.
- **Risks:** Increased reliance on third-party models for national security; potential data leakage via CRM integrations; dependency on rapidly changing proprietary API standards.
- **Opportunities:** Significant efficiency gains in sales and legal workflows; reduced operational costs with GPT-6.2; ability to move sensitive workloads to local infrastructure (llama.cpp).
- **Recommended Actions:** 
  1. Audit current sales and legal workflows for potential AI integration. 
  2. Evaluate local model deployment (via llama.cpp) for data-sensitive internal processes.
  3. Monitor "Astra" performance in enterprise environments to replace legacy automation tools.

---

## Technology Trends

*   **Vertical Specialization:** AI is moving away from generic chatbots toward "Vertical AI," where models are purpose-built for specific professional tasks (e.g., Law, Sales).
*   **Edge/Local Sovereignty:** The push for local model execution (llama.cpp) indicates a strong market desire to reduce cloud dependency and increase data privacy.
*   **Infrastructure Interoperability:** Tools like OpenRouter and LLMON suggest a trend toward making AI models "plug-and-play," reducing the technical burden on developers.

---

## Terminology

*   **Open-Weight Models:** AI models where the underlying file (the "weights") is available for anyone to download and run on their own hardware, as opposed to closed models like GPT-4.
*   **CRM (Customer Relationship Management):** Software used by companies to manage interactions with current and potential customers.
*   **SimReady Assets:** Pre-built 3D digital objects that act like their real-world counterparts, used for training robots in a virtual environment.
*   **Local AI:** Running AI models on your own computer or servers rather than sending data to an external provider's cloud.
*   **Markup Language:** A system for annotating a document in a way that is syntactically distinguishable from the text (like HTML), now being repurposed for AI to understand complex instructions.
