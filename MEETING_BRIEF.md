# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 Generation:** The jump to GPT-6 (Astra/Sol) marks a new baseline for reasoning and computer interaction.
*   **Proactive Agents:** The launch of "dots" moves the industry toward autonomous, long-running project support.
*   **Standardization:** Significant moves by the UK AISI toward reproducible benchmarks and OpenAI’s "Safety Cases."

### Risks
*   **Operational Interference:** As seen in Australia, AI agents can inadvertently disrupt government or corporate web infrastructure if not properly throttled.
*   **High Complexity:** Managing "dots" and autonomous agents requires new oversight frameworks to prevent "shadow" work or unintended outcomes.

### Opportunities
*   **Cost Optimization:** GPT-6.1 Sol allows for a 5x reduction in costs for coding and computer-use tasks compared to flagship models.
*   **Legacy Software Automation:** Models like Holo4 allow companies to automate tasks in old software systems that lack modern APIs.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Cost Audit:** Evaluate current GPT-4/5 usage and identify tasks (especially coding) that can be migrated to **GPT-6.1 Sol** to save costs.
2.  **Agent Pilot:** Launch a small-scale pilot using **"dots"** for non-critical project management to test proactive AI workflows.
3.  **Compliance Alignment:** Review internal AI training and deployment against the new **"Safety Case"** framework to prepare for future regulation.

---

## Technology Trends

1.  **Computer-Use (Action Models):** The focus is shifting from AI that "talks" to AI that "does," interacting directly with user interfaces.
2.  **Model Bifurcation:** The release of Astra (power) vs. Sol (efficiency) shows that the industry is splitting into "super-intelligence" for research and "workhorse" models for production.
3.  **Proactive Autonomy:** The move from reactive prompting to proactive task management (e.g., dots).
4.  **Verification and Citations:** Increasing technical rigor in ensuring AI agents can prove where their information comes from (Source-Aware Verification).

---

## Terminology

*   **GPT-6 Astra:** OpenAI’s newest, most powerful flagship AI model.
*   **GPT-6.1 Sol:** A "mini" version of GPT-6 optimized for speed, cost, and specific tasks like coding.
*   **Dots:** Proactive AI agents that can work on projects independently over long periods.
*   **Computer-Use Agent:** AI that can control a computer (moving the mouse, clicking, typing) just like a human.
*   **Safety Case:** A structured argument, supported by evidence, that a system is safe to operate in its intended environment.
*   **Tabular Prediction:** AI models used to predict outcomes based on data organized in tables (like Excel files).
*   **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standard that helps AI models connect to and understand the context of various data sources.
*   **Reproducibility:** The ability for an independent team to run the same test on an AI and get the same results, ensuring the AI’s performance isn't being exaggerated.
