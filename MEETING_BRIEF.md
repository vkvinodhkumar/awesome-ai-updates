# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Scientific Reasoning:** AI is now solving theoretical math problems, not just writing emails.
- **Hardware Agnosticism:** New encoders are making long-context AI viable on CPUs.
- **Agentic Risk:** We are seeing the first documented technical timelines of AI agent-led security intrusions.

### Risks
- **Cyber-Sovereignty:** Stricter EU regulations may limit deployment speed for non-compliant models.
- **Automated Fraud:** Criminal use of LLMs for "pig butchering" scams is increasing in sophistication.
- **Resource Waste:** Companies are losing significant capital on idle GPU compute.

### Opportunities
- **Cost Reduction:** Leveraging "Abundant Intelligence" frameworks to lower API and operational costs.
- **Geospatial Insights:** Utilizing platforms like OlmoEarth for ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) reporting and supply chain monitoring.
- **Workforce Transformation:** Following the Univé model to upskill employees and drive "bottom-up" innovation.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Audit Compute:** Evaluate internal GPU utilization to eliminate "idle" costs.
2. **Review Compliance:** Ensure all EU-facing AI products are prepared for the EU AI Act’s transparency requirements.
3. **Security Patching:** Update security protocols to account for "Agentic" threats—AI that can execute code or move through networks autonomously.

---

## Technology Trends

1. **The Rise of Reasoning (System 2 Thinking):** AI is moving beyond "next-token prediction" toward "slow thinking," where the model pauses to reason through complex math and logic.
2. **Planetary AI:** Using LLMs and Transformers to interpret non-text data, specifically geospatial and satellite imagery.
3. **Efficiency over Raw Power:** A trend toward "Liquid" or CPU-based inference suggests that the industry is looking for alternatives to the high-cost GPU monopoly.
4. **Agentic Security:** As AI agents become more autonomous, the field of "AI Red Teaming" is shifting from "content safety" to "network security."

## Terminology

- **Theoretical Computer Science (TCS):** The study of the mathematical foundations of computing, including what can and cannot be computed efficiently.
- **Provenance:** The origin and history of a piece of data; in AI, this often refers to tools that prove a photo or text was/wasn't generated by AI.
- **Abundant Intelligence:** A concept where the cost of AI reasoning drops so low that it can be applied to every minor task without financial concern.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to make sense of location-based data, such as identifying a type of crop from a satellite photo.
- **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "remember" and process a massive amount of information (like a 500-page book) in one go.
- **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create a realistic, interactive virtual world for robots or humans to train in.
- **Agent Intrusion:** A security breach where an autonomous AI agent is used (knowingly or via exploit) to infiltrate a secure computer system.
