# Executive Meeting Brief

- **Key Developments:** Rise of "Computer Use" agents; shift from LLMs to "reasoning" models in math/science; emphasis on enterprise-grade synthetic data.
- **Risks:** High reliance on "agentic" systems that fail to cross-reference their own work with databases; increasing regulatory pressure on provenance and watermarking.
- **Opportunities:** Significant efficiency gains in project management (Atlassian) and legal/financial research (Ironclad/Jump); competitive advantage through fine-tuned reasoning models.
- **Recommended Actions:** 
    1. Audit current manual workflows for automation potential using "Agentic" workflows.
    2. Review data privacy policies in preparation for mandatory AI-provenance/watermarking standards.
    3. Shift investment focus from general LLM chat tools to specialized, benchmarked models for technical tasks.

---

## Technology Trends

1. **Reasoning-as-a-Service:** AI is evolving from predicting the next word to proving the next step in logic-heavy fields like math and law.
2. **Agentic Verification:** Moving from "Generative AI" (create something) to "Agentic AI" (do something and verify it worked).
3. **Synthetic Data Dependency:** Enterprises are increasingly generating their own training data to bypass privacy constraints and data scarcity.

## Terminology

- **Agentic Workflow:** A process where an AI is given a goal and can independently perform multiple steps, use tools, and make decisions to finish it.
- **Computer Use:** AI capability that allows a model to operate a computer by clicking, typing, and navigating applications like a human.
- **Frontier Models:** The most powerful, state-of-the-art AI models currently available.
- **Lean:** A programming language used specifically to write and verify mathematical proofs.
- **Provenance:** The documentation of the origin of a piece of digital content; in AI, knowing if text or media was generated by a machine.
- **Synthetic Data:** Data that is created by AI models rather than collected from human behavior, used to train or refine other models.
- **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into human-like audio.
