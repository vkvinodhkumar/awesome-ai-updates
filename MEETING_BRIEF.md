# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Cost Efficiency:** GPT-5.6 (Luna/Terra) and CPU-based encoders are drastically lowering the cost of "thinking."
*   **Geospatial AI:** AI is now capable of planetary-scale physical monitoring.
*   **Regulatory Alignment:** Major providers are now "compliance-first" to secure the European market.

### Risks
*   **Adversarial AI:** Criminal organizations are successfully using LLMs for high-volume social engineering.
*   **Agent Security:** Autonomous agents introduce new vectors for system intrusions that traditional firewalls may not catch.
*   **Compute Waste:** Mismanaged GPU resources are leading to significant "hidden" costs in R&D.

### Opportunities
*   **Enterprise Upskilling:** Following the Univé model to turn employees into AI developers via low-code/no-code enterprise tools.
*   **Specialized Robotics:** Opportunities in high-stakes fields like surgery and geospatial analysis where general models fall short.
*   **Standard Hardware:** Utilizing LFM2.5-style encoders to run AI on existing CPU infrastructure to save on cloud costs.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Compute:** Review current GPU utilization to identify "grounded" (idle) resources.
2.  **Evaluate GPT-5.6:** Assess if moving high-volume workflows to the Luna or Terra models can reduce operational costs.
3.  **Update Security Protocols:** Brief cybersecurity teams on the specific risks of "Agent Intrusion" and AI-driven social engineering.
4.  **Governance Check:** Ensure all AI deployments align with the upcoming EU AI Act standards to avoid future retrofitting.

## Technology Trends
*   **The Race to the Bottom (Price):** Innovation is currently focused as much on *cost* as it is on *capability*.
*   **Full-Stack Optimization:** The move from "just a model" to integrated hardware-software-governance stacks.
*   **Physical World Inference:** AI is moving from digital-only (chat) to physical-world applications (geospatial and surgical robotics).

## Terminology
*   **Data Provenance:** The record of where data originated and how it has moved/changed over time; vital for legal and ethical AI.
*   **Price-Performance Frontier:** The balance between the cost of a model and its intelligence level; the "sweet spot" for business value.
*   **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to "understand" and make predictions based on geographic and satellite data.
*   **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "read" and remember a very large amount of information (like a whole book) in one go.
*   **Agent Intrusion:** A security breach where an autonomous AI agent is used (or hijacked) to perform unauthorized actions on a network.
*   **Full-Stack AI:** An approach where a company controls everything from the hardware and data to the final user application.
