# Executive Meeting Brief

- **Key Developments:** OpenAI is successfully applying AI to the "hard sciences" (Math/CS). Simultaneously, Liquid AI is proving that high-performance inference can happen on cheaper CPU hardware.
- **Risks:** Malicious actors are increasingly using LLMs for social engineering scams. Additionally, "Agentic" systems introduce new cybersecurity vulnerabilities that traditional firewalls may not catch.
- **Opportunities:** There is a massive opportunity in "Physical AI" and specialized domains like surgical robotics (NVIDIA) and geospatial monitoring (AI2). 
- **Recommended Actions:** 
    1. **Resource Audit:** Review GPU utilization metrics to eliminate "idle" costs. 
    2. **Capability Shift:** Explore CPU-based encoders for long-context internal document analysis to save on API/GPU costs. 
    3. **Governance:** Implement a "Univé-style" internal training program to ensure the workforce is AI-literate before deploying agentic tools.

## Technology Trends

1. **Hardware Diversification:** A move away from GPU-only reliance toward CPU-optimized inference for specific tasks.
2. **Abundant Intelligence:** A structural industry shift toward making tokens "too cheap to meter."
3. **Agentic Security:** A new focus on the "Intrusion Detection" of autonomous AI agents.
4. **Synthetic Training Environments:** Using generative AI to create simulations for physical robots (healthcare/manufacturing).

## Terminology

- **System 2 Thinking:** In AI, this refers to slow, deliberate reasoning (like solving a math problem) rather than quick, probabilistic word prediction.
- **Provenance:** The tracking of the origin and history of data or content, often used to identify if an image or text was AI-generated.
- **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "read" and remember a massive amount of information (like a 500-page book) in a single session.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to analyze map data, satellite imagery, and location-based information.
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that don't just answer questions but can autonomously use tools, browse the web, or execute software to complete a goal.
- **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create realistic, "fake" environments for robots to practice in, similar to a flight simulator for pilots.
