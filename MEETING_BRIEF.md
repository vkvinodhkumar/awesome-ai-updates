# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **GPT-6 Astra Launch:** The arrival of Astra marks a significant increase in reasoning accuracy, particularly in finance and software engineering.
*   **The $1B Security Pivot:** OpenAI’s "Daybreak" initiative suggests that cybersecurity is now the primary battlefield for AI supremacy.

### Risks
*   **Model Capability Thresholds:** GPT-6 Astra reaching the "Critical" level in cybersecurity suggests that the model could be misused for sophisticated hacking if not strictly governed.
*   **Dependency:** As businesses like Playco and Legora see 40-50% efficiency gains, the risk of "model lock-in" and dependency on a single provider increases.

### Opportunities
*   **Workflow Compression:** The ATV Big Air case study shows that tasks previously requiring 72 hours can now be completed in 3 hours, offering a massive competitive advantage for early adopters.
*   **Edge Deployment:** New fine-tuning techniques (GRPO) allow for high-performing, small-scale models that can run locally and securely.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Workflows:** Identify "high-friction" document review or prototyping tasks for a GPT-6 Astra pilot.
2.  **Evaluate Cybersecurity:** Review current defense protocols in light of the new "Critical" capabilities of frontier models.
3.  **Explore Small-Model Fine-tuning:** Investigate using GRPO to create specialized, low-cost models for internal structured data tasks.

---

## Technology Trends
1.  **From Chatbots to Agents:** The focus is shifting toward models with "Memory" and the ability to execute multi-step workflows (e.g., photos-to-website).
2.  **Defensive AI Scaling:** Massive capital is being deployed to ensure AI acts as a shield for infrastructure rather than just a productivity tool.
3.  **Efficiency over Size:** Research is trending toward making smaller models (like the 350M parameter model) perform as accurately as large ones through smarter training (GRPO).

## Terminology

*   **GPT-6 Astra:** The latest, most advanced AI model from OpenAI, noted for high reasoning capabilities.
*   **Frontier Cyber AI:** Advanced AI tools specifically designed for high-level cybersecurity tasks like threat detection and mitigation.
*   **Preparedness Framework:** A set of safety guidelines used to measure the risks (cyber, biological, etc.) posed by advanced AI models.
*   **Multimodal-native:** An AI system built from the ground up to understand multiple types of data (text, images, audio) simultaneously.
*   **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** An efficient way to "teach" an AI model by comparing several of its answers at once and rewarding the best one.
*   **Time Series Models:** AI models specifically designed to look at data over time to predict future trends (e.g., stock prices or weather).
*   **TRL (Transformer Reinforcement Learning):** A method of training AI by giving it feedback on its actions to improve its performance over time.
