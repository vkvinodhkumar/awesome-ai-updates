# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
The release of GPT-5.6 and the "Ultrafast" tier indicates that the bottleneck for AI is shifting from "intelligence" to "latency." Furthermore, the shift toward "Agentic AI" means models are now being trusted to perform actions, not just generate text.

### Risks
- **Reproducibility:** The ICML study suggests that a significant portion of AI research can be difficult to verify; companies must perform due diligence on "bleeding edge" models.
- **Cost Management:** While GPT-5.6 is more efficient, the move toward agentic AI (which runs in loops) can lead to unexpected token consumption if not monitored.

### Opportunities
- **Edge Deployment:** New models like LFM2.5-VL-3B allow for sophisticated AI on-site without cloud latency or data privacy risks.
- **Autonomous Workflows:** The transition from "assistance to execution" allows for the automation of middle-office operations.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Assess Latency Needs:** Evaluate if "Ultrafast" tiers can enable new customer-facing products that were previously too slow.
2. **Audit for Agency:** Identify departmental processes where AI can move from "suggesting" to "executing" (e.g., automated bug fixing or procurement).
3. **Explore Specialized Embeddings:** For environmental or spatial projects, pivot from general-purpose models to specialized ones like OlmoEarth.

---

## Technology Trends

1. **Sub-second Latency:** The push for 700+ tokens per second is making AI feel human-instant.
2. **Agentic Shift:** A move away from "Chat" interfaces toward "Agent" interfaces that perform tasks in the background.
3. **Token Efficiency:** Researchers (IBM, OpenAI) are finding ways to get more "logic" out of fewer "tokens," driving down costs.
4. **Physical AI:** Convergence of LLMs with robotics (LeRobot) is accelerating the path to general-purpose robots.

---

## Terminology

- **Agentic AI:** AI that can independently perform a series of tasks to achieve a goal, rather than just answering questions.
- **Tokens per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI generates text. Higher TPS means faster responses.
- **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like text or images) as numbers so an AI can compare and understand relationships between them.
- **Edge AI:** Running AI models directly on local devices (phones, sensors, robots) rather than in a centralized data center.
- **Responses API:** A tool for developers that simplifies how an AI model returns information, making it easier to build apps.
- **Reproducibility:** The ability for an independent team to achieve the same results as the original researchers using the same methods.
