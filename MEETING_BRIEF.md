# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
*   **Commercial Scaling:** OpenAI is maturing its leadership (CRO) and revenue streams (Ads).
*   **Agentic Shift:** AI is moving from "writing" to "doing" (Agentic AI).
*   **Specialization:** New models are targeting specific niches like Cybersecurity (Daybreak) and Earth Science (OlmoEarth).

### Risks
*   **User Experience:** Introducing ads to ChatGPT may alienate users or raise concerns about the "neutrality" of AI responses.
*   **Frontier Gap:** Firms failing to move toward "execution-based" AI risk falling permanently behind competitors who are automating core workflows.

### Opportunities
*   **Edge Deployment:** New models from Liquid AI and NVIDIA allow for sophisticated AI on local devices without high cloud costs.
*   **Cost Reduction:** Techniques like knowledge distillation and token reduction offer a path to significantly lower AI operational expenses.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Audit Workflows:** Move beyond "GenAI as a search engine" and identify processes suitable for "Agentic AI" (execution).
2.  **Evaluate Edge AI:** Explore whether high-latency cloud models can be replaced with low-latency edge models (like LFM or Magpie) for customer-facing or internal tools.
3.  **Security Review:** Test the Daybreak models via AWS Bedrock to enhance internal cybersecurity posture.

---

## Technology Trends

1.  **Monetization Diversification:** AI providers are moving away from purely subscription-based models toward ad-supported and specialized enterprise models.
2.  **Model Distillation & Efficiency:** There is a concentrated effort to make AI smaller and cheaper without sacrificing intelligence.
3.  **Agentic AI:** A shift from "Human-in-the-loop" to "AI-as-the-agent," where the model autonomously completes tasks across different software.

## Terminology

*   **Chief Revenue Officer (CRO):** An executive responsible for all revenue-generating processes in a company.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI that doesn't just talk but can take actions, like booking a flight, writing code, or managing an inbox.
*   **Ads in ChatGPT:** Commercial content placed in the AI interface to generate revenue.
*   **AWS Bedrock:** A service from Amazon that makes it easy to build and scale generative AI applications using various models.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like words or images) as numbers so an AI can understand the relationship between different concepts.
*   **Edge AI:** AI that runs locally on a device (like a phone or a sensor) rather than on a massive server in the cloud.
*   **Tokens:** The basic units (words or parts of words) that AI models process. Fewer tokens mean lower costs.
*   **Knowledge Distillation:** A technique where a large, smart "Teacher" model trains a smaller, faster "Student" model to perform at a similar level.
*   **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Technology that converts written text into natural-sounding human speech.
