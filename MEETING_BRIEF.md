# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **The Speed Breakthrough:** OpenAI’s "Ultrafast" mode (750 tokens/sec) removes the "lag" barrier for AI agents.
- **Agentic Shift:** Industry focus has officially moved from "Chat" to "Execution."
- **Edge Dominance:** New 3B-parameter models (LFM) are making sophisticated vision-language tasks possible on local hardware.

### Risks
- **Widening Performance Gap:** Companies not adopting "agentic" workflows risk falling behind "frontier firms" who are automating 10x faster.
- **Reproducibility:** A significant portion of AI research remains difficult to replicate, necessitating caution when adopting "cutting-edge" unproven techniques.

### Opportunities
- **Hardware-Software Synergy:** Utilizing specialized tiers (like Sol/Cerebras) for customer-facing applications that require instant feedback.
- **Robotics Integration:** Using the LeRobot framework to start experimenting with physical automation in logistics or manufacturing.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Audit Current AI Usage:** Transition from "assistance" (summarization) to "execution" (agents) in internal workflows.
2. **Evaluate Sol API:** Pilot the GPT-5.6 Sol "Ultrafast" tier for real-time customer service or internal engineering tools.
3. **Explore Open Models:** Review the Summer 2026 Open Model report to determine if proprietary model costs can be reduced by switching to open-source alternatives for specific tasks.

## Technology Trends
- **The Velocity Era:** AI is moving toward "instant" generation, enabling seamless human-AI collaboration.
- **Embodied AI:** The integration of AI agents with robotics (LeRobot) is maturing, moving AI out of the screen and into the physical world.
- **Model Specialization:** Rather than one model for everything, we are seeing "Earth" models, "Vision" models for the edge, and "Sol" models for speed.

## Terminology

- **Agentic AI:** AI that doesn't just suggest answers but takes actions (e.g., booking a flight or writing and running code) to complete a goal.
- **Tokens per Second (TPS):** The speed at which an AI generates text. Higher TPS means faster responses.
- **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like words or images) as numbers so an AI can understand the relationship between different concepts.
- **Edge AI:** Running AI models directly on a local device (like a smartphone or a sensor) instead of on a massive remote server.
- **Vision-Language Model (VLM):** An AI that can "see" and "read" simultaneously, allowing it to describe images or follow visual instructions.
- **Liquid Foundation Models (LFM):** A type of AI model designed to be more efficient and adaptable in processing continuous data streams, often better for smaller devices.
