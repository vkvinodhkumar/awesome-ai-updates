# Executive Meeting Brief

- **Key Developments:** The launch of GPT-5.6 and the "Ultrafast" Sol tier represent a massive leap in both reasoning quality and execution speed (750 tps).
- **Risks:** The move toward "Agentic AI" (AI that executes tasks) introduces new security risks regarding autonomous data access and unintended actions. Additionally, the rapid pace of model releases threatens to make recently built infrastructure obsolete.
- **Opportunities:** Significant cost savings in engineering (via Codex/ChatGPT Work) and the ability to deploy "Edge AI" for physical operations using Liquid AI’s new vision models.
- **Recommended Actions:** 
    1. Pilot the GPT-5.6 Responses API for one internal "agentic" workflow (e.g., automated customer support or internal reporting).
    2. Evaluate the "Ultrafast" tier for latency-sensitive customer-facing applications.
    3. Review data governance policies to ensure they account for autonomous agents that "execute" rather than just "assist."

## Technology Trends

1.  **Agentic Shift:** AI is evolving from a tool you talk to into a worker that performs multi-step tasks autonomously.
2.  **Hyper-Inference:** Specialized hardware (like Cerebras) is making AI response times nearly instantaneous, removing the "typing" lag.
3.  **Embodied AI:** The integration of robotics (LeRobot) with cloud storage indicates a push toward AI that interacts with the physical world.
4.  **Edge Specialization:** Smaller, high-performance models (like LFM2.5) are enabling complex AI features on local devices without cloud dependency.

## Terminology

- **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized version of the GPT-5.6 model optimized for extreme speed and high-volume output.
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that can independently plan, use tools, and execute multi-step tasks to achieve a goal.
- **Tokens Per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI generates text; higher TPS means faster responses.
- **Responses API:** A tool for developers that manages how an AI chooses the best model or path to answer a query.
- **Embeddings:** A way of converting data (like text or climate data) into numbers that an AI can understand and compare.
- **Edge AI:** AI that runs locally on a device (like a phone or a robot) rather than on a distant server.
- **Liquid Foundation Model (LFM):** A type of AI architecture designed to be more efficient and adaptable than traditional models, particularly for time-sensitive data.
