# Executive Meeting Brief

### Key Developments
- **Vertical Integration:** OpenAI is no longer just a model company; they are now a hardware and full-stack infrastructure company.
- **Incremental Model Gains:** GPT-5.6 is focusing on efficiency and developer utility rather than just a "larger" parameter count.
- **Model Compression:** Quantization techniques are reaching a level where compressed models are as good as full-size ones.

### Risks
- **Disinformation Scalability:** State actors (Russia) are actively using LLMs to create sophisticated, fake intellectual fronts.
- **Benchmark Gaming:** Over-reliance on standardized tests (especially in Speech/ASR) may lead to purchasing software that performs poorly in real-world noisy environments.

### Opportunities
- **Cost Reduction:** Custom chips (Jalapeño) and 4-bit quantization offer a path to significantly lower operational costs for AI implementations.
- **Rapid Prototyping:** Tools like Gradio and Kiro allow for faster deployment of internal AI tools with less engineering overhead.

### Recommended Actions
1. **Infrastructure Audit:** Review if current AI projects could be moved to more efficient 4-bit models to save on cloud costs.
2. **Security Update:** Brief communications and IT teams on the nature of AI-generated covert influence campaigns to better spot "fake" industry research.
3. **Vendor Evaluation:** When procuring Speech AI, demand "real-world" testing data rather than just standard benchmark scores.

## Technology Trends
1. **Custom Silicon:** Major AI players are building their own chips to bypass the "NVIDIA tax."
2. **The "Middle Path" Models:** Models like GPT-5.6 and Granite 4.2 focus on being "better and cheaper" rather than just "bigger."
3. **Agentic Workflows:** Moving away from a single chat prompt toward automated "workflows" that handle planning, building, and testing.

## Terminology

- **Inference:** The process of a trained AI model actually running and providing an answer to a user.
- **Throughput:** A measure of how much data an AI system can process in a specific amount of time.
- **Latency:** The delay or "lag" between a user's input and the AI's response.
- **Full Stack:** In AI, this refers to owning everything from the physical hardware (chips) to the software models and the final user app.
- **Quantization:** A compression technique that reduces the mathematical precision of an AI model to make it smaller and faster.
- **4-bit:** A specific level of high compression for AI models; standard models usually run at 16-bit or 32-bit.
- **Covert Influence Campaign:** A coordinated effort by a group (often a government) to spread propaganda while hiding who is actually behind the message.
- **Benchmark Optimization:** The practice of "teaching to the test," where an AI is tuned specifically to get a high score on a specific test, even if it doesn't improve its actual usefulness.
