## Technology Trends
*   **Agentic Reliability:** A move toward "Thinking" models that can verify their own work against a database.
*   **Synthetic Data Generation:** Using AI to create the data needed to train even better AI.
*   **Specialization vs. Generalization:** The rise of specialized models for specific tasks like report writing (AstaBrief) or tabular data (Kumo).

## Terminology
*   **Text Provenance:** The ability to track the origin of a piece of text (e.g., whether it was written by a human or an AI).
*   **Tabular Data:** Data organized in rows and columns, like a spreadsheet or a SQL table.
*   **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Technology that converts written text into audible human speech.
*   **Voice Cloning:** Using AI to recreate the specific tone, pitch, and accent of a specific person's voice.
*   **Synthetic Data:** Data that is artificially generated by an AI model rather than collected from real-world events, used to train other models.
*   **Agentic Workflow:** An AI system designed to not just "talk," but to execute a series of tasks or actions in other software.
