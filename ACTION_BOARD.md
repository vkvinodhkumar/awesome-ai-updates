## Technology Trends
*   **The Rise of the "Agent":** The industry is obsessed with models that "do" rather than just "say."
*   **Compute Efficiency:** Partnerships between model providers (OpenAI) and specialized chipmakers (Cerebras) are becoming the standard for performance.
*   **Open-Source Maturity:** Open models are moving away from "generalist" goals toward specialized niches like robotics and geospatial analysis.

## Terminology
*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized variant of the GPT-5.6 model optimized for extreme speed and low-latency responses.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI systems capable of planning and executing multi-step tasks autonomously.
*   **Tokens Per Second (TPS):** The speed at which an AI generates text; higher TPS allows for more human-like interaction speeds.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like text or images) as numbers so that an AI can understand the relationships between different pieces of information.
*   **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A new type of AI architecture that is often more efficient at processing sequential data and running on smaller hardware than traditional "Transformers."
*   **LeRobot:** An open-source library specifically designed for training and controlling robotic systems using AI.
*   **Edge Computing:** Running AI models directly on local devices (like a smartphone or a factory sensor) rather than on a central cloud server.
