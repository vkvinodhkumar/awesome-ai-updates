## Terminology
*   **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** A privacy setting where the AI provider does not store or save any of the data sent to the model for training or review.
*   **Inference:** The process of an AI model actually running and providing an answer (as opposed to "training").
*   **Late Interaction:** A sophisticated way for AI to search for information that looks at multiple parts of a sentence at once, rather than just one general summary.
*   **GPU Utilization:** A measure of how much of a computer's "AI brain" (Graphics Processing Unit) is actually being used; higher is better and more cost-effective.
*   **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** Technology that converts spoken language into text.
*   **Multi-Vector Embeddings:** A way of turning text into complex numbers so that an AI can understand the relationship between different ideas more accurately.
