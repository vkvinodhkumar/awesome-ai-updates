## Technology Trends
*   **Edge AI & Small Models:** Moving away from "bigger is better" toward "small and efficient" models that run on local devices.
*   **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create digital twins and training environments for robotics (e.g., NVIDIA Cosmos).
*   **AI Agency:** A transition from chatbots that "talk" to agents that "do" (performing tasks in software or physical reality).

## Terminology
*   **Astra:** An OpenAI project focused on advanced, multimodal AI capabilities and safety evaluations.
*   **Inference:** The process of a trained AI model making a prediction or generating a response based on new input data.
*   **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI model architecture designed for continuous data streams and high efficiency.
*   **GPU Orchestration:** The automated management of computer chips (Graphics Processing Units) to ensure they are working efficiently and not sitting idle.
*   **Productive Struggle:** An educational concept where a learner faces a challenge that is difficult but solvable, leading to deeper understanding.
*   **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create realistic, dynamic environments (like a digital operating room) for training other machines.
