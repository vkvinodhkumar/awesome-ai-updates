## Technology Trends
- **The "Free" Tier Shift:** Companies are moving away from charging for tokens and toward ad-supported or "Freemium" models to capture market share.
- **Inference Speed as a Moat:** The focus is shifting from "how smart is the model" to "how fast and cheap is the model."
- **Democratized Software:** AI is turning "product managers" into "developers," significantly shortening the software development life cycle (SDLC).

## Terminology

- **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** A privacy setting where the AI provider does not store any of the data sent to the model after the response is generated.
- **GPT-5.6 Luna:** A hypothetical/advanced iteration of the Generative Pre-trained Transformer focused on efficient, high-performance software generation.
- **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI architecture designed for continuous-time data processing, often resulting in much faster inference speeds.
- **Multi-Vector / Late Interaction:** A method of searching where the AI compares multiple parts of a question to multiple parts of an answer at the same time, leading to much higher accuracy than standard search.
- **Inference:** The process of an AI model actually "thinking" and generating a response to a prompt.
- **GPU Utilization:** A measure of how much of a computer's "brain power" (Graphic Processing Unit) is actually being used; higher utilization means less wasted money.
