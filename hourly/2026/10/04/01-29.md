## Terminology

- **GPT-6 / GPT-5.6:** Next-generation Large Language Models (LLMs) from OpenAI featuring enhanced reasoning and logic capabilities.
- **Reasoning Effort:** A setting that allows a user to control how much computational time/power a model spends "thinking" before providing an answer.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standardized way for AI models to connect to external data sources and tools securely.
- **Synthetic Training Data:** Artificial data created by AI that mimics the characteristics of real-world data, used to train other models.
- **Tabular Prediction:** The use of AI to analyze and predict outcomes based on data organized in rows and columns (spreadsheets).
- **Voice Cloning:** The process of using AI to create a synthetic version of a specific human's voice.
- **Codex:** A specialized AI model designed to understand and generate computer code.
