## Terminology

*   **GPT-6 (Reasoning Effort):** The latest model family where users can potentially specify how much computational "thought" or time the model should spend on a complex problem.
*   **Codex:** A specialized AI model designed to understand and generate computer code.
*   **Synthetic Data:** Information that is artificially manufactured by an AI rather than generated by real-world events, used to train other AI models safely.
*   **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into natural-sounding spoken audio.
*   **Tabular Data:** Data organized in a table (rows and columns), such as an Excel spreadsheet or a SQL database.
*   **MCP Agents:** AI agents using the "Model Context Protocol," which allows different AI tools and data sources to talk to each other seamlessly.
*   **Source-Aware Verification:** A process where an AI double-checks its answers against original documents to ensure accuracy and provide citations.
