## Technology Trends
*   **Tunable Reasoning:** The ability to tell a model how much "effort" to put into a specific prompt, balancing cost vs. intelligence.
*   **Agentic Verification:** Moving from models that just "chat" to agents that "do," with external systems verifying their work.
*   **Synthetic-First Training:** A shift toward training enterprise AI on high-quality, artificially generated data rather than messy, private real-world data.
*   **Multimodal Ad Tech:** The integration of visual ads directly into AI conversational flows.

## Terminology
*   **Text Provenance:** The ability to track the origin of a piece of text to prove whether it was written by a human or an AI.
*   **Reasoning Effort:** A setting in next-gen models (like GPT-6) that dictates how many "thinking steps" the AI takes before answering.
*   **Synthetic Data:** Data that is generated by an AI model rather than collected from real-world events, used to train other AI models.
*   **Tabular Data:** Structured data organized in rows and columns, like a spreadsheet or a database table.
*   **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Technology that converts written text into spoken audio, often involving "voice cloning" to mimic a specific person.
*   **Agentic Workflow:** A process where an AI "agent" is given a goal and independently chooses which tools and steps to take to achieve it.
