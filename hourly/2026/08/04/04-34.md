## Terminology

- **Turnless Speech:** AI that listens and talks simultaneously, recognizing when to stop or start based on natural conversation cues rather than a "submit" button.
- **Geospatial Inference:** The process of using AI to "understand" and make predictions based on map, satellite, and location data.
- **LFM (Liquid Foundation Models):** A type of AI model designed to be more efficient and adaptable than traditional Transformers, often capable of running on weaker hardware.
- **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "read" and remember a massive amount of information (like a 500-page book) in one go.
- **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create a "digital twin" or fake environment that looks and acts like the real world to train robots.
- **EU AI Act:** A set of new laws in Europe that categorizes AI systems by risk and mandates transparency and safety.
