## Terminology

- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** A key business metric measuring how much money a company makes per customer.
- **Turnless Speech:** A voice AI model that allows for natural interruptions and overlapping speech, rather than waiting for a specific "turn" to talk.
- **Low-latency:** Refers to a system that responds almost instantly, with very little delay.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to analyze and make predictions based on satellite images or geographic data.
- **GPU Management:** The practice of scheduling and optimizing the use of Graphics Processing Units to ensure they aren't sitting idle.
- **Long-Context:** The ability of an AI to "remember" or process a very large amount of information (like a whole book) in a single session.
- **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI model designed for high efficiency and continuous data processing.
- **Agent Intrusion:** A security event where an autonomous AI agent is manipulated or "hacked" to perform unauthorized tasks.
