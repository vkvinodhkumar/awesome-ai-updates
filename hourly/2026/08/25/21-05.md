## Terminology

-   **Inference:** The process of an AI model running and providing an output (the "using" phase of AI, as opposed to the "training" phase).
-   **Throughput:** The amount of data or number of queries an AI system can process in a specific amount of time.
-   **Latency:** The delay or "lag" between sending a prompt and receiving a response from the AI.
-   **Quantization:** A compression technique that reduces the precision of a model's numbers (e.g., from 16-bit to 4-bit) to make it run faster and use less memory.
-   **4-bit Model:** A model that has been compressed significantly. Think of it like a low-resolution image that still manages to look as clear as a high-resolution one.
-   **Full-Precision:** A model running at its original, uncompressed mathematical detail.
-   **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** Technology that converts spoken language into text.
-   **Price-Performance:** A metric measuring how much "intelligence" or work you get for every dollar spent on AI tokens or computing.
