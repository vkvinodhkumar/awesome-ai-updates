## Terminology

-   **Agentic AI:** AI that can plan and execute multi-step tasks autonomously rather than just answering questions.
-   **Codex:** A specialized AI model designed to understand and generate computer code.
-   **Knowledge Distillation:** The process of transferring the "knowledge" of a massive, expensive AI model into a smaller, faster, and cheaper model.
-   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (like text or images) as numbers so that an AI can understand the relationships between different pieces of information.
-   **Edge AI:** Running AI models locally on a device (like a phone or a factory sensor) instead of in a distant cloud data center.
-   **Token:** The basic unit of text that an AI processes (roughly 4 characters or 0.75 words).
-   **TTS (Text-to-Speech):** Technology that converts written text into natural-sounding human speech.
-   **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI architecture designed for high efficiency and continuous data processing.
