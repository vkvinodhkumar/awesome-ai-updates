## Terminology

- **System 2 Reasoning:** A psychological term applied to AI, referring to slow, deliberate, and logical thinking (as opposed to fast, intuitive "System 1" pattern matching).
- **Provenance:** The origin or source of something; in AI, this refers to tracking the data used to train a model or generate an output.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to analyze and draw conclusions from geographic data, such as satellite imagery or GPS coordinates.
- **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to process and understand a massive amount of information (like a whole book or a large database) in a single session.
- **Digital Twin:** A virtual representation of a physical object or system, used here to train robots in a simulated environment.
- **Agent Intrusion:** A cyber-attack carried out by an autonomous AI "agent" that can navigate systems and make decisions without human input.
- **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI model designed for high efficiency and continuous data processing, often requiring less compute than traditional models.
