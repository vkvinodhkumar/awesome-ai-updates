## Terminology

- **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized version of the GPT-5.6 model optimized for extreme speed and high-volume output.
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that can independently plan, use tools, and execute multi-step tasks to achieve a goal.
- **Tokens Per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI generates text; higher TPS means faster responses.
- **Responses API:** A tool for developers that manages how an AI chooses the best model or path to answer a query.
- **Embeddings:** A way of converting data (like text or climate data) into numbers that an AI can understand and compare.
- **Edge AI:** AI that runs locally on a device (like a phone or a robot) rather than on a distant server.
- **Liquid Foundation Model (LFM):** A type of AI architecture designed to be more efficient and adaptable than traditional models, particularly for time-sensitive data.
