## Terminology

- **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition):** Technology that converts spoken language into text.
- **EHR (Electronic Health Record):** A digital version of a patient’s paper chart, highly regulated for privacy.
- **Frontier Model:** The most advanced class of AI models that push the boundaries of current capabilities.
- **WebGPU:** A modern technology that allows web browsers to use a computer’s graphics card to run complex tasks like AI very quickly.
- **Multi-Vector Embedding:** A way of organizing information so an AI can find very specific, related pieces of data more accurately.
- **Time Series Model:** An AI model specifically designed to predict future values based on past data collected over time (e.g., stock prices or weather).
- **Data Contamination:** When an AI model "cheats" on a test because it already saw the answers in its training data.
