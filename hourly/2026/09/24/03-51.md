## Terminology

*   **Quantization:** A method of reducing the precision of the numbers in an AI model (e.g., from 16-bit to 4-bit) to make it smaller and faster without losing much intelligence.
*   **Pruning:** Removing "unnecessary" connections or blocks from an AI model to make it lighter and faster to run.
*   **Benchmarks:** Standardized tests used to measure how well an AI model performs on specific tasks.
*   **Ising Optimization:** A technique borrowed from statistical physics used to solve complex problems by looking at how different parts of a system interact.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI that can perform tasks and make decisions independently to reach a goal, rather than just answering questions.
