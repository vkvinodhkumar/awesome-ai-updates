## Terminology

- **GPT-6 Astra:** A new, high-capability AI model designed for reasoning and autonomous system management.
- **Fine-tuning:** The process of taking a pre-trained AI and giving it extra training on a smaller, specific dataset to change its behavior or style.
- **Habitat:** OpenAI's custom-built storage system used to handle the data for over a billion users.
- **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** A method for training AI that helps it learn better by comparing a group of possible answers against each other.
- **LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation):** A technique to train AI models using much less computer memory, making it cheaper and faster.
- **NCCL:** A standard tool used by computers to talk to each other very quickly during AI training; "No NCCL" means the training can happen on slower, more common networks.
- **Time Series:** Data that is recorded over time, like daily stock prices or hourly temperature readings.
- **Multimodal:** An AI's ability to understand and process different types of input, like text, images, and video, at the same time.
