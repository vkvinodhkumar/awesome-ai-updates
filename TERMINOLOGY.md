# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-08-21 16:22:51

* **Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** A privacy framework where an AI provider guarantees that any data sent to their models via API is processed in-memory and immediately deleted, never being saved to disk or used for future model training.
* **Inference:** The process of a trained AI model running live data to make a prediction, solve a problem, or generate text/images for an end-user.
* **Liquid Foundation Models (LFMs):** A class of AI models built on alternative mathematical frameworks (inspired by biological brains) that can adapt to new data over time and require significantly less computational power than traditional Transformer models.
* **Multi-Vector / Late Interaction Models:** A search technology that keeps track of the meaning of individual words (tokens) within a document, rather than combining the whole document into one average summary. This allows for incredibly precise search results.
* **GPU Cluster Utilization:** A metric representing how actively a network of graphics processing units (GPUs) is being used. Higher utilization means less wasted power, less idle time, and lower overall operational costs.
* **Token:** A chunk of text (ranging from a single character to a word) that an AI model uses as its basic unit of processing. AI usage costs are typically calculated based on the number of input and output tokens.
