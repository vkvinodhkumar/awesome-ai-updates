# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-08-25 16:26:51

- **Inference:** The process of a trained AI model actually running and providing an answer to a user.
- **Throughput:** A measure of how much data an AI system can process in a specific amount of time.
- **Latency:** The delay or "lag" between a user's input and the AI's response.
- **Full Stack:** In AI, this refers to owning everything from the physical hardware (chips) to the software models and the final user app.
- **Quantization:** A compression technique that reduces the mathematical precision of an AI model to make it smaller and faster.
- **4-bit:** A specific level of high compression for AI models; standard models usually run at 16-bit or 32-bit.
- **Covert Influence Campaign:** A coordinated effort by a group (often a government) to spread propaganda while hiding who is actually behind the message.
- **Benchmark Optimization:** The practice of "teaching to the test," where an AI is tuned specifically to get a high score on a specific test, even if it doesn't improve its actual usefulness.
