# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-07-31 17:24:47

- **GPT-5.6 (Luna/Terra):** The latest iteration of OpenAI's models, optimized for specific speed and cost requirements.
- **Geospatial Inference:** The process of using AI to draw conclusions or find patterns in satellite imagery and geographic data.
- **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "remember" and process very large amounts of information (like a 500-page book) in a single session.
- **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create realistic, "fake" environments (like a digital operating room) where other AI or robots can learn.
- **GPU Orchestration:** The management of computer chips (GPUs) to ensure they are working efficiently and not sitting idle.
- **Agentic Intrusion:** A security breach where an autonomous AI agent (rather than a human) performs the hack.
