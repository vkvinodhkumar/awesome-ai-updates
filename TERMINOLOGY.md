# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-08-02 22:47:43

- **System 2 Thinking:** In AI, this refers to slow, deliberate reasoning (like solving a math problem) rather than quick, probabilistic word prediction.
- **Provenance:** The tracking of the origin and history of data or content, often used to identify if an image or text was AI-generated.
- **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "read" and remember a massive amount of information (like a 500-page book) in a single session.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to analyze map data, satellite imagery, and location-based information.
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that don't just answer questions but can autonomously use tools, browse the web, or execute software to complete a goal.
- **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create realistic, "fake" environments for robots to practice in, similar to a flight simulator for pilots.
