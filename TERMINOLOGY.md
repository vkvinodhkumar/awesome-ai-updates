# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-07-27 16:10:33

*   **4-bit Diffusion Inference:** A method of reducing the "size" of a model so it can run faster and use less memory while producing high-quality images.
*   **Frontier AI:** The most advanced, cutting-edge AI models that currently exist and are pushing the boundaries of what is possible.
*   **Quantization:** The process of compressing an AI model to make it smaller and faster without losing significant accuracy.
*   **Generative Simulation:** AI that creates realistic "fake" environments to help train robots or systems in situations that are too dangerous or expensive to test in the real world.
