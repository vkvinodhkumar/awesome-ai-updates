# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-08-15 05:18:31

*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized version of GPT-5 optimized for extreme speed and low-latency responses.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI systems that don't just process text but can actively execute tasks and navigate software like a human would.
*   **Tokens per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI generates text. 750 TPS is roughly equivalent to reading several pages of text in one second.
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing data (text, images, or scientific data) as numbers so that an AI can understand the relationships between different pieces of information.
*   **Edge AI:** Running AI models locally on devices (like phones or sensors) rather than in a giant remote data center.
*   **Liquid Foundation Model (LFM):** A type of AI architecture (different from traditional Transformers) designed to be more efficient at processing continuous data.
