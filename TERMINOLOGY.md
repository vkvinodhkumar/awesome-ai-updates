# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-07-31 20:55:53

- **Provenance:** The origin and history of a piece of data or a model; used to prove that AI-generated content is authentic or follows specific rules.
- **Full-stack AI:** An approach where a company controls everything from the hardware and data to the model and the user interface.
- **Long-context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "read" and remember a very large amount of information (like a 500-page book) in a single session.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to identify patterns and make predictions based on geographic and satellite data.
- **Frontier Lab Agent:** A highly advanced, semi-autonomous AI system capable of performing complex multi-step tasks with minimal human intervention.
- **Luna and Terra:** Specific model configurations or "sizes" within the OpenAI GPT-5 ecosystem, likely optimized for different levels of speed and complexity.
