# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-08-13 20:32:53

*   **GPT-5.6 Sol:** The latest iteration of OpenAI's model, optimized for speed and "agentic" (task-performing) capabilities.
*   **Tokens per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI produces text. Higher TPS results in faster, more human-like responses.
*   **Agentic AI:** AI systems that don't just answer questions but can actually perform tasks (e.g., booking a flight, writing and executing code, or managing an inbox).
*   **Embeddings:** A way of representing complex data (like images or climate patterns) as numbers so an AI can understand and compare them.
*   **Edge AI:** Running AI models directly on local devices (like a camera or phone) rather than in a giant data center.
*   **Codex:** An AI model specifically fine-tuned to understand and write computer programming code.
*   **Liquid Foundation Model (LFM):** A type of AI model that can adapt its behavior more fluidly than traditional models, often requiring less computing power.
