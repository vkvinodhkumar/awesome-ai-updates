# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-08-03 06:12:19

- **Theoretical Computer Science (TCS):** The study of the mathematical foundations of computing, including what can and cannot be computed efficiently.
- **Provenance:** The origin and history of a piece of data; in AI, this often refers to tools that prove a photo or text was/wasn't generated by AI.
- **Abundant Intelligence:** A concept where the cost of AI reasoning drops so low that it can be applied to every minor task without financial concern.
- **Geospatial Inference:** Using AI to make sense of location-based data, such as identifying a type of crop from a satellite photo.
- **Long-Context Inference:** The ability of an AI to "remember" and process a massive amount of information (like a 500-page book) in one go.
- **Generative Simulation:** Using AI to create a realistic, interactive virtual world for robots or humans to train in.
- **Agent Intrusion:** A security breach where an autonomous AI agent is used (knowingly or via exploit) to infiltrate a secure computer system.
