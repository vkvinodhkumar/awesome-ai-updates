# AI Terminology

Last Updated: 2026-08-16 17:12:50

- **GPT-5.6 Sol:** A specialized version of OpenAI's model optimized for high-speed "inference" (generating answers).
- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that can take actions (like sending emails or moving files) rather than just generating text.
- **Tokens per Second (TPS):** A measure of how fast an AI can generate words or characters; higher is faster.
- **Embeddings:** A way of turning data (like words or images) into numbers so a computer can understand the relationship between them.
- **Edge Capabilities:** The ability of an AI to run on local hardware (like a phone or a robot) without needing the internet.
- **LFM (Liquid Foundation Model):** A type of AI model that is more flexible and efficient at processing continuous data than traditional models.
- **Responses API:** A tool for developers that makes it easier to get structured, reliable answers from an AI model.
